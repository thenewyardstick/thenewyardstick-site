Mitsubishi Guide

Mitsubishi has re-established itself in the UK with a smaller but more focused range, concentrating on practical family vehicles and dependable workhorses. The Outlander PHEV builds on the brand's long-standing expertise in plug-in hybrid technology, while the L200 remains one of Britain's best-known pickup trucks.

Although the line-up is currently more limited than many rivals, Mitsubishi continues to offer strong reliability, generous equipment and excellent towing capability. With a renewed commitment to the UK market, the brand remains a sensible choice for buyers prioritising practicality, durability and dependable everyday motoring.

Current Models

Mitsubishi Outlander (26-)

RRP From : £46,995
Pre Reg From : c. £42,500
Used Price From : c. £42,000

Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4,720 × 1,862 × 1,751 mm
Boot : 495 litres, 183 litres with 7 seats in use

Key rivals : Hyundai Santa Fe, Jaecoo 8, Kia Sorento, Peugeot 5008, Volkswagen Tayron

The latest Outlander continues Mitsubishi's pioneering approach to plug-in hybrid technology, combining genuine electric-only capability with the practicality of a spacious family SUV. Standard four-wheel drive, an available seven-seat layout and generous equipment make it an appealing choice for families. Pricing now places the Outlander closer to more premium rivals than previous generations, making Mitsubishi's reputation for reliability and strong standard specification more important than ever.

Mitsubishi L200 (26-)

RRP From : £43,554 inc. VAT 
Pre Reg From : c. £41,500 inc. VAT
Used Price From : c. £41,500 inc. VAT

Powertrains : Diesel
Dimensions : 5,320 × 1,865 × 1,795 mm
Load Bed : 1,555 × 1,545 × 526 mm

Key rivals : Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max, KGM Musso, Maxus T60 Max, Toyota Hilux

The Mitsubishi L200 remains one of Britain's best-known pickup trucks, combining strong off-road ability with a 3,500kg braked towing capacity and a payload of more than one tonne. The latest generation builds on that reputation with improved refinement, modern safety technology and a more comfortable cabin, while its 2.4-litre bi-turbo diesel and available Super Select 4WD system retain the rugged capability that has made the L200 a favourite with tradespeople, farmers and adventure buyers alike.
