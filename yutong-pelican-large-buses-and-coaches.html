Yutong E12 & U11DD

Yutong's E12 and U11DD form the backbone of its larger electric bus offering in Britain, providing zero-tailpipe-emission alternatives to conventional diesel single- and double-deckers. The 12.2-metre E12 accommodates 39 seated passengers and up to 70 overall, making it suitable for busy urban and regional routes. The shorter 10.9-metre U11DD instead uses a double-deck layout with up to 70 seats and a maximum total capacity of 84. Both use lithium iron phosphate batteries and Yutong's integrated electric safety systems, with substantial battery capacity supporting intensive daily operation. Full air conditioning, accessible passenger accommodation and configurable interiors make them suitable for British public transport operators.

Yutong GTe14

The GTe14 is Yutong's flagship electric coach for British operators, combining zero-tailpipe-emission travel with the passenger comfort and luggage capacity expected of a full-sized touring vehicle. Its three-axle, 14-metre body accommodates up to 57 passengers, with an accessible 53-seat configuration also available. A substantial 563kWh battery provides a claimed range of around 300 miles, while an optional 621kWh battery offers additional capacity. Twin CCS charging connections allow charging at up to 300kW, making the GTe14 suitable for scheduled intercity journeys, airport services and longer-distance private hire where charging facilities can be planned. Reclining seats, air conditioning, luggage accommodation and modern passenger equipment complete the package.

Yutong TC9 (2026 Generation)

The TC9 has become one of Britain's most familiar midicoaches, offering full touring-coach comfort within a compact 9.5-metre body. An extensively updated generation arrived for 2026, introducing revised exterior styling, a redesigned dashboard and upgraded seats inspired by the larger GT12. A Cummins 6.7-litre diesel engine producing 320bhp is paired with a ZF automatic gearbox, while full air suspension contributes to passenger comfort. Operators can choose 37 seats or a 34-seat layout with an onboard toilet, with up to 5.8 cubic metres of luggage capacity. Its relatively compact dimensions make the TC9 particularly suitable for private hire, excursions, schools and routes where larger coaches would be difficult to manoeuvre.

Yutong GT12 & GT14

The GT12 and GT14 form Yutong's full-sized diesel coach range for Britain, combining established European engines and transmissions with comfortable, extensively equipped passenger interiors. The two-axle GT12 offers 53 seats, powered by a 450bhp DAF diesel engine, and has become a familiar sight on British touring and intercity services. The newer three-axle GT14 arrived in 2025, extending seating capacity to 61 passengers and introducing a more powerful 482bhp DAF engine. Both provide reclining seats, USB charging, air conditioning, onboard toilets and extensive luggage accommodation. The larger GT14 also benefits from a standard camera mirror system and 360-degree cameras, making it particularly attractive for operators requiring greater capacity and long-distance capability.

Important Information

Prices, specifications, dimensions, seating capacities, weights, power outputs and equipment are based primarily on information published by vehicle manufacturers, their official UK representatives and established specialist suppliers. Specifications can vary by body length, drivetrain, interior configuration, model year and individual vehicle.

Prices shown are UK prices where available and are intended as a guide. Where an official UK list price is unavailable, pricing is shown as POA. Prices may vary according to fleet order size, optional equipment, delivery charges, accessibility requirements and operator-specific configurations.

Passenger seating capacities must not be confused with total passenger capacities. Urban buses accommodate standing passengers, while wheelchair spaces, tip-up seating, onboard toilets and other interior equipment may alter the number of available seats. Maximum seating figures refer to selected configurations and exclude the driver.

Electric battery capacities, charging rates and driving ranges are manufacturer figures based on stated conditions. Real-world range depends on passenger load, route characteristics, temperature, heating, vehicle speed and battery condition. Optional batteries and charging equipment can affect range and charging performance.

Licence categories shown are a general guide only. Individual driving entitlement can vary according to passenger-seat count, vehicle weight, vehicle length, licence issue date, grandfather rights, Section 19 operation and other exemptions. Passenger-seat counts used for licence purposes exclude the driver. Always check the vehicle's plated specification and your own driving-licence entitlement before driving.

Models are included on the basis of current British distributor listings and published UK-market information. Older vehicles, overseas-only models and specialist airport buses are excluded from this selection. Individual specifications, registration approval, pricing and delivery dates should be confirmed with Pelican Bus & Coach.

Specifications are checked against the latest information reasonably available to The New Yardstick at the time of publication or update. Manufacturers and distributors may alter specifications, pricing, equipment or model availability without notice.
