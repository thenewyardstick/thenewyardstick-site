Ineos Guide

Ineos Automotive is the vehicle-making arm of the wider Ineos industrial group, created to develop a rugged off-roader for buyers who want traditional mechanical capability. The Grenadier was engineered with specialist partners, uses BMW six-cylinder engines and is manufactured at Hambach in France.

Its appeal centres on demanding outdoor use, towing and travel beyond well-surfaced roads, rather than the polished road manners of a typical luxury SUV. The five-seat Station Wagon and Quartermaster double-cab pickup are covered here, with their closely related trims grouped together.

The Grenadier is a deliberately traditional off-roader, built around a separate chassis, permanent four-wheel drive and substantial mechanical hardware. Its cabin mixes practical controls with modern conveniences, while Fieldmaster, Trialmaster and Black Edition versions give the same basic vehicle different emphases. It suits buyers who will use its capability, towing ability and durability rather than simply admire the rugged shape.

The Quartermaster combines the Grenadier’s traditional off-road engineering with a separate pickup bed and a longer wheelbase. It is a specialist alternative to mainstream double cabs, aimed at owners who value rough-terrain ability and a substantial mechanical feel. Fieldmaster, Trialmaster and Black Edition versions add different equipment, while the petrol and diesel engines both use an automatic gearbox.
