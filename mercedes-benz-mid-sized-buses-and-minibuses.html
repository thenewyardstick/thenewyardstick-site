Mercedes-Benz / Tremonia Midibuses



Mercedes-Benz / Tremonia Sprinter Transfer 35

The Sprinter Transfer 35 provides a practical bridge between conventional passenger vans and larger purpose-built minibuses. Based on the rear-wheel-drive Mercedes-Benz Sprinter, its approximately 6.9-metre body accommodates up to 19 passengers in selected configurations. It is particularly well suited to school transport, private hire, community services and local shuttle operations. Operators can specify different seating arrangements, passenger air conditioning, luggage equipment and access solutions, allowing the Transfer 35 to fulfil a wide variety of duties without requiring a larger vehicle.

Mercedes-Benz / Tremonia Sprinter Transfer 45

The larger Sprinter Transfer 45 offers greater passenger capacity and a broader selection of interiors, making it suitable for everything from school transport to comfortable private-hire work. Its 7.4-metre body can accommodate up to 22 passengers in school-bus specification, while touring and shuttle versions typically provide up to 19 more generously spaced seats. Reclining seats, luggage accommodation and alternative entrance arrangements give the Transfer 45 considerable versatility. The combination of Mercedes-Benz diesel power and automatic transmission also makes it well suited to intensive commercial use.

Mercedes-Benz / Tremonia Sprinter City 45

The Sprinter City 45 is a compact low-entry bus designed for scheduled services where a conventional single-decker would be unnecessarily large. Its narrow 2.02-metre body makes it particularly suitable for historic town centres, rural routes and densely populated residential areas. A low-floor section provides accessible accommodation for wheelchairs and pushchairs, while the raised rear seating area maximises interior space. Depending on its approval and seating arrangement, the City 45 can accommodate up to 28 passengers, including standing passengers, making it considerably more versatile than a conventional minibus.

Mercedes-Benz / Tremonia Sprinter City 75

The Sprinter City 75 represents the largest diesel-powered member of Tremonia's compact urban bus family. Measuring approximately 8.5 metres long, it bridges the gap between conventional minibuses and larger single-deck buses, accommodating up to 38 passengers in mixed seating and standing configurations. Its distinctive body incorporates a generous low-floor area, accessible passenger entrances and a specialised heavy-duty rear axle. Despite its substantial capacity, the narrow body makes it particularly useful on routes where a conventional bus would struggle with restricted roads or tight corners.

Tremonia City 45 Electric

The City 45 Electric brings zero-emission operation to Tremonia's compact low-entry city bus platform. Retaining the narrow body and accessible passenger area of the diesel Sprinter City 45, it uses a substantial 126kWh battery system and an electric drivetrain designed for repeated urban operation. Tremonia quotes up to approximately 187 miles under favourable SORT test conditions, although actual range will depend heavily on route, passenger load and weather. With space for up to 22 passengers, the City 45 Electric is particularly suited to smaller public transport routes, community services and town-centre operations where electric running is increasingly desirable.

Tremonia City 75 Electric

The City 75 Electric is Tremonia's largest battery-electric midibus, bringing zero-emission operation to a vehicle approaching the capacity of a conventional urban single-decker. Its 8.5-metre body provides accommodation for up to 34 passengers, including standing space, while a generous low-floor area allows wheelchair and pushchair access. A 126kWh battery system and rapid DC charging support intensive urban operation, with approximately 186 miles claimed under favourable conditions. Named Minibus of the Year 2025, the City 75 Electric offers an interesting alternative for operators seeking lower-capacity electric buses without moving to a full-sized vehicle.

Tremonia Transfer 45 Electric (New for 2026)

The newly introduced Transfer 45 Electric expands Tremonia's battery-electric range beyond low-entry city buses into conventional passenger transport. Based on the familiar Transfer 45 format, it combines a 160kW electric motor with a 126kWh battery system, providing zero-emission operation for school transport, shuttle services and private hire. Seating and entrance arrangements can be adapted to different operator requirements, with capacity for up to 22 passengers. Compared with the low-floor City models, the Transfer is designed around seated passenger comfort, making it particularly interesting for operators seeking an electric alternative to diesel-powered Sprinter minibuses.

Important Information

Prices, specifications, dimensions, seating capacities, weights, power outputs and equipment are based primarily on information published by vehicle manufacturers, their official representatives and established specialist converters. Specifications can vary by trim, body length, configuration, conversion, model year and individual vehicle. Tremonia Mobility is an independent manufacturer producing specialist buses using Mercedes-Benz vehicle platforms and mechanical components.

Prices shown are UK prices where available and are intended as a guide. Where an official UK list price is unavailable, pricing may be shown as approximate or POA. Prices can change and may exclude optional equipment, conversions, delivery charges or other operator-specific costs. International model availability does not guarantee confirmed UK supply.

Passenger capacities on low-entry City models may include standing passengers. The maximum passenger capacity must not be confused with the number of available seats. Seating arrangements, standing allowances and wheelchair accommodation vary according to specification and vehicle approval.

Licence categories shown are a general guide only. Individual driving entitlement can vary according to passenger-seat count, vehicle weight, vehicle length, licence issue date, grandfather rights, Section 19 operation and other exemptions. Passenger-seat counts used for licence purposes exclude the driver. Always check the vehicle's plated specification and your own driving-licence entitlement before driving.

Electric driving ranges are manufacturer claims based on stated test conditions, including SORT bus-duty testing where specified, and should not be treated as directly comparable with passenger-car WLTP figures. Real-world range will depend on passenger load, speed, temperature, heating, route characteristics and operating conditions.

Specifications are checked against the latest information reasonably available to The New Yardstick at the time of publication or update. Manufacturers and converters may alter specifications, pricing or model availability without notice.
