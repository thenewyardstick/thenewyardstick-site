MG Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

MG has transformed from a historic British sports car maker into one of the UK's fastest-growing car brands. Now owned by SAIC Motor, the range focuses on value for money, generous equipment and increasingly advanced hybrid and electric technology.

Models such as the MG3 Hybrid+, MG HS and award-winning MG4 have helped the brand become a serious alternative to established European, Japanese and Korean rivals. With competitive prices, a seven-year warranty and improving quality, MG offers everything from affordable superminis to sports cars, hatchbacks and practical family SUVs, making it one of the strongest value-focused brands on sale today.

It is an excellent choice for buyers seeking low running costs without moving to a fully electric car.

The original MG4 remains one of the UK's best electric hatchbacks, combining rear-wheel drive handling, competitive range and excellent value.
