Toyota Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Toyota has built its reputation on reliability, efficiency and exceptional long-term ownership costs, becoming the brand that many buyers trust above all others. Today, that reputation is supported by one of the industry's broadest line-ups, spanning city cars, family hatchbacks, SUVs, pick-ups, vans and an expanding range of electric vehicles. Hybrid technology remains at the heart of the brand, but Toyota is increasingly embracing battery electric models while continuing to refine the dependable engineering that has made it one of the world's best-selling manufacturers.

Current Models

Toyota Aygo X (22-)

RRP From : £21,645
Pre Reg From : c. £19,000
Used Price From : c. £7,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 3776 × 1740 × 1525 mm
Boot : 231L

Key rivals : Fiat 500, Hyundai i10, Kia Picanto, Mini Cooper, Suzuki Swift

The Aygo X combines city car running costs with SUV-inspired styling and a higher driving position. Toyota's new hybrid powertrain brings a significant boost in performance alongside impressive fuel economy, making the Aygo X feel much more confident on faster roads while remaining one of the most efficient petrol-powered city cars on sale.

Toyota Yaris (20-)

RRP From : £23,995
Pre Reg From : c. £19,000
Used Price From : c. £10,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 3940 × 1745 × 1500 mm
Boot : 286L

Key rivals : Fiat Grande Panda, Hyundai i20, MG3, Peugeot 208, Renault Clio

The Yaris continues to set the benchmark for hybrid superminis. Outstanding fuel economy, compact dimensions and Toyota’s renowned reliability have helped it remain one of Europe’s most successful small cars, while generous standard safety equipment strengthens its appeal. Some rivals do offer a larger boot, but overall usability remains a strong point.

Toyota GR Yaris (20-)

RRP From : £51,995
Pre Reg From : c. £51,995
Used Price From : c. £22,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 3995 × 1805 × 1455 mm
Boot : 174L

Key rivals : Alpine a290, MG 4 XPower, Mini Cooper JCW, Peugeot E-208 GTi, Vauxhall Corsa GSE

The GR Yaris remains one of the purest driver's cars on sale. Developed with genuine World Rally Championship influence, its turbocharged engine, sophisticated four-wheel-drive system and lightweight construction deliver an experience few modern performance cars can match. As more small performance cars move towards electrification or disappear altogether, the GR Yaris has become one of the last true petrol-powered hot hatch icons.

Toyota Yaris Cross (21-)

RRP From : £27,445
Pre Reg From : c. £23,500
Used Price From : c. £11,500

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4180 × 1765 × 1595 mm
Boot : 397L

The Yaris Cross combines the efficiency of Toyota's hybrid technology with the practicality and higher driving position buyers increasingly demand. Compact outside yet surprisingly spacious inside, it has become one of Europe's strongest-selling small SUVs.

Pre Reg From : c. £25,500
Used Price From : c. £23,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4285 × 1800 × 1635 mm

Key rivals : Jeep Avenger, Kia EV3, Renault 4, Vauxhall Frontera Electric, Volvo EX30

The Urban Cruiser marks Toyota's expansion into the compact electric SUV market. Competitive electric range, practical dimensions and Toyota's reputation for dependability should make it a compelling alternative for buyers making the switch to electric.

Toyota Corolla (19-)

RRP From : £30,845
Pre Reg From : c. £22,000
Used Price From : c. £10,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4370 × 1790 × 1460 mm
Boot : 361L, 596L Touring Sports

Key rivals : Dacia Striker, Kia K4, Mazda3, Peugeot 308, SEAT Leon

The Corolla remains one of the world’s best‑known family cars for good reason. Efficient hybrid powertrains, strong refinement and a choice of hatchback or Touring Sports estate body styles make it a smart long‑term ownership proposition. The Touring Sports is also available as a van, with a durable load bay that broadens its appeal to more buyers.

Toyota Proace City Verso (24-)

RRP From : £30,495
Pre Reg From : c. £21,000
Used Price From : c. £20,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4403, 4753 XL × 1848 × 1812 mm
Boot : 775L, 1050L XL

Key rivals : Citroën Berlingo, Ford Tourneo Courier, Peugeot Rifter, Renault Kangoo, Volkswagen Caddy

The Proace City Verso prioritises practicality above all else, offering sliding rear doors, flexible seating and one of the largest boots in the compact MPV class. Available with up to seven seats and two body lengths, it delivers family-friendly versatility that few SUVs can match, while its all-electric powertrain makes it an appealing option for buyers wanting maximum space with low running costs.

Toyota Proace Verso (18-)

RRP From : £45,895
Pre Reg From : c. £42,000
Used Price From : c. £14,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4981, 5331 XL × 1924 × 1910 mm
Boot : 989L, 1300L XL

Key rivals : Fiat E-Ulysse, Kia PV5, Lexus LM, Mercedes-Benz V-Class, Vauxhall Vivaro Life

The Proace Verso is one of the UK’s most practical passenger vehicles, offering seating for up to nine occupants without compromising comfort or luggage space. Available in two body lengths, it blends generous passenger accommodation with genuine long‑distance capability, suiting large families, executive transport and hotel shuttle use. Like the smaller Proace City, it’s available with up to 10 years of warranty cover.

Toyota Prius (23-)

RRP From : £37,895
Pre Reg From : c. £27,500
Used Price From : c. £21,000

Powertrains : Plug-in Hybrid
Dimensions : 4599 × 1782 × 1430 mm
Boot : 284L

Key rivals : DS No4 PHEV, Mercedes-Benz A250e, Peugeot 408 PHEV, Renault Rafale PHEV, Volkswagen Golf PHEV

The latest Prius transforms Toyota’s hybrid icon into a genuinely stylish fastback. Improved electric‑only range, stronger performance and eye‑catching design make it one of the most efficient plug‑in hybrids currently available. Strong pre‑reg deals add to its appeal, though a similar‑sized Corolla remains the more practical choice overall.

Toyota C-HR (24-)

RRP From : £31,695
Pre Reg From : c. £23,000
Used Price From : c. £19,000

Powertrains : Hybrid, PHEV
Dimensions : 4362 × 1832 × 1564 mm
Boot : 388L, 310L PHEV

Key rivals : Hyundai Kona, Kia Niro, Mazda CX-30, Peugeot 3008, Renault Symbioz

The second-generation C-HR builds on the success of its predecessor with sharper styling, a more premium cabin and one of the broadest electrified powertrain ranges in the class. It remains one of Toyota's most distinctive family SUVs.

Toyota C-HR+ (26-)

RRP From : £32,995
Pre Reg From : c. £32,000
Used Price From : C. £28,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4530 × 1870 × 1595 mm
Boot : 416L

Key rivals : Leapmotor B10, Nissan Leaf, Peugeot E-3008, Renault Scénic E-Tech, Volkswagen ID.5

The C-HR+ expands Toyota's electric line-up with a larger, more practical alternative to the hybrid C-HR. Built on a dedicated EV platform, it offers significantly longer electric range, faster charging and a more spacious cabin, while retaining the bold styling that has made the C-HR such a success. Sitting neatly between the Urban Cruiser and bZ4X, it gives buyers another compelling electric SUV option without moving into premium-brand pricing.

Toyota bZ4X (22-)

RRP From : £38,495
Pre Reg From : c. £30,000
Used Price From : c. £17,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4690 × 1860 × 1650 mm
Boot : 452L

Key rivals : Jeep Compass Electric, Kia EV5, Leapmotor C10, MG S6, Nissan Ariya

The bZ4X has matured into a far stronger proposition thanks to continual improvements in charging, software and battery technology. Spacious, comfortable and increasingly competitive on price, it forms the backbone of Toyota’s growing electric SUV range. Its biggest challenge is the CH‑R+, which offers a similar package with greater range for less money.

 Toyota RAV4 (26-)

RRP From : £43,845
Pre Reg From : c. £38,345
Used Price From : c. £38,345

Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4600 × 1855 × 1700 mm
Boot : 446L

Key rivals : Ford Kuga, Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Vauxhall Grandland, Volkswagen Tiguan

The RAV4 continues to define the practical family SUV, offering impressive efficiency and one of the strongest plug‑in hybrid systems on the market. It remains an outstanding choice for mixed electric and longer‑distance use, and its reputation as a hugely reliable large SUV makes it a standout in the PHEV SUV crowd.

Toyota Land Cruiser (24-)

RRP From : £74,995
Pre Reg From : c. £65,000
Used Price From : c. £54,000

Powertrains : Diesel, MHEV
Dimensions : 4925 × 1980 × 1935 mm
Boot : 742L, 136L 7-Seat

Key rivals : Ineos Grenadier, Land Rover Defender, Mercedes-Benz G-Class

The latest Land Cruiser combines genuine off‑road capability with far greater refinement than previous generations. Built to tackle the toughest conditions while remaining comfortable on the road, it continues to set the benchmark for durability and dependability. The 76 Series adds retro appeal, while commercial versions broaden its usefulness for businesses needing serious off‑road ability.

Toyota Hilux (25-)

RRP From : £34,640
Pre Reg From : c. £33,000
Used Price From : c. £27,000

Powertrains : Diesel, MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 5325 × 1855 × 1815 mm
Boot : Pickup

Key rivals : Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max, KGM Musso, Maxus T90 EV, Volkswagen Amarok

The Hilux has earned a legendary reputation for toughness, and the latest model builds on that with improved refinement, updated technology and electrified powertrains. It remains one of the most dependable pick-ups available for both work and recreation.
