Toyota Guide

Toyota has built its reputation on reliability, efficiency and low long-term ownership costs, becoming one of the world's largest and most trusted car manufacturers. Today that reputation is supported by an unusually broad range spanning compact hybrids, family cars, SUVs, performance models, people carriers, pick-ups and an expanding line-up of fully electric vehicles.

Hybrid technology remains central to the brand, but Toyota's electric range is growing rapidly with the Urban Cruiser, C-HR+, bZ4X and larger bZ4X Touring. At the same time, plug-in hybrid, mild-hybrid and conventional combustion technology continue to give buyers a particularly wide choice of powertrains.

Toyota Land Cruiser (24-)

Dimensions : 4925 × 1980 × 1935 mm

The latest Land Cruiser combines serious off-road ability with considerably greater refinement than earlier generations. Durability and genuine off-road engineering remain at the heart of its appeal.

Powertrains : Diesel MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 5325 × 1855 × 1815 mm
Boot : Pickup

Key rivals : Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max, KGM Musso, Maxus T90 EV, Volkswagen Amarok

The ninth-generation Hilux moves Toyota's famous pick-up into a new era, with a 48V mild-hybrid diesel joined by the first fully electric Hilux. Diesel versions retain a 3,500kg towing capacity and payload of more than one tonne, while the electric model provides a zero-emission alternative for buyers whose work allows its shorter range and lower payload.
