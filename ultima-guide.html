Ultima Guide

Ultima is one of Britain's most uncompromising specialist sports car names, best known for producing extraordinarily lightweight, mid-engined machines with immense V8 performance. Rather than following conventional supercar manufacturers, the company developed its cars around a spaceframe chassis, driver-focused engineering and the option of customer assembly.

Its latest design, the RS, combines Le Mans-inspired styling with a choice of Chevrolet V8 engines and performance extending well beyond that of many considerably more expensive supercars.

Ultima entered a new chapter in August 2026 when American company Peregryn acquired the brand's intellectual property, rights and tooling. Existing UK orders remain in progress, but future production is planned in the United States from 2027. Consequently, new UK pricing and availability should be confirmed directly with the new manufacturer rather than assumed from historical UK kit prices.

Key rivals: Ariel Atom, BAC Mono, Caterham Seven, Ginetta Akula, Noble M500

The RS is an exceptionally lightweight two-seat supercar with a mid-mounted Chevrolet V8, rear-wheel drive and a six-speed manual transaxle. Engine choice and specification vary between builds, with outputs ranging from roughly 480bhp to as much as 1,200bhp in highly tuned versions. Weighing around 930kg in lightweight specification, it places acceleration, cornering ability and direct driver involvement ahead of everyday practicality. UK examples are exceptionally rare, and their prices vary significantly according to engine, assembly quality and equipment.
