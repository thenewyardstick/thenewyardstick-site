Nissan Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Nissan's Primastar Combi sits at the smaller end of the passenger-transport market, combining the practicality of a mid-sized van with seating for up to nine people. Its removable rear seating and useful luggage capacity make it suitable for larger families, sports clubs, hotel transfers, travel companies and private-hire work without stepping up to a larger conventional minibus.

Nissan Primastar Combi

The Primastar Combi is the passenger version of Nissan's mid-sized van, offering three rows of seating for up to nine occupants while retaining useful luggage space behind the rear seats. The rear seating can be adjusted or removed to trade passenger capacity for cargo space, making the Primastar particularly versatile for airport transfers, sports teams, travel companies and larger families. Its relatively compact dimensions and car-like cab also make it less intimidating to drive than a full-size minibus.
