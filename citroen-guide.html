Citroën Guide

Citroën has reinvented itself as one of Europe's leading value-focused car brands, offering distinctive styling, generous equipment and family-friendly practicality at competitive prices.

From the tiny Ami quadricycle to practical family SUVs and MPVs, the range prioritises comfort and affordability over outright performance. Many models are now available with petrol, hybrid or electric powertrains, while clever packaging and keen pricing help Citroën stand out in an increasingly crowded market.

Current Models

Citroën Ami (20-)

RRP From: £7,695
Pre Reg From: c. £6,000
Used Price From: c. £4,500

Powertrains: EV
Dimensions: 2410 × 1390 × 1520 mm
Boot: 63L

Key rivals: Fiat Topolino, Mobilize Duo, Silence S04

The Ami is unlike anything else on sale, offering affordable electric mobility for short urban journeys. Classified as a quadricycle rather than a conventional car, it's compact, inexpensive to run and remarkably easy to park. It won't suit longer trips, but for city centres or private estates it makes plenty of sense.

Citroën C3 (24-)

RRP From: £18,495
Pre Reg From: c. £15,000
Used Price From: c. £13,000

Powertrains: Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions: 4015 × 1755 × 1577 mm
Boot: 310L

Key rivals: Dacia Sandero Stepway, Fiat Grande Panda, MG3 Hybrid+, Renault Clio, Toyota Yaris

The latest C3 blends supermini dimensions with a crossover-inspired driving position, generous equipment and excellent value. Available with petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains, it offers an attractive alternative to more expensive rivals while placing comfort and everyday usability ahead of sporty handling.

Citroën C3 Aircross (24-)

RRP From: £21,475
Pre Reg From: c. £18,000
Used Price From: c. £15,500

Powertrains: Petrol, Hybrid, EV
Dimensions: 4395 × 1795 × 1660 mm
Boot: 460L (40L 7-seat)

Key rivals: Dacia Bigster, MG ZS, Renault Symbioz, Skoda Kamiq, Vauxhall Frontera

The C3 Aircross offers impressive interior space and, uniquely in its class, the option of seven seats. Generous standard equipment, efficient hybrid and electric powertrains and competitive pricing make it one of the strongest value-focused family SUVs, even if some rivals offer sharper handling.

Citroën C4 (20-) 

RRP From: £24,815
Pre Reg From: c. £18,500
Used Price From: c. £8,000

Powertrains: Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions: 4350 mm × 1800 × 1525 mm
Boot: 380L 

Key rivals: Kia K4 / EV4, Mazda 3, MG4 Urban, Renault Mégane E-Tech, Seat Leon

The C4 combines hatchback practicality with crossover-inspired styling, prioritising comfort, distinctive design and everyday usability over outright sportiness. Available with petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains, it offers a relaxed and versatile alternative to more conventional family hatchbacks while retaining the raised stance and softer character that define Citroën’s current range.

Citroën C5 Aircross (25-)

RRP From: £31,030
Pre Reg From: c. £25,000
Used Price From: c. £25,000

Powertrains: MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions: 4652 × 1902 × 1688 mm
Boot: 565L

Key rivals: Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Peugeot 3008, Renault Austral, Volkswagen Tiguan

The latest C5 Aircross is Citroën's flagship family SUV, offering generous passenger space, a comfortable ride and an upmarket interior. Hybrid, plug-in hybrid and electric powertrains broaden its appeal, while competitive pricing makes it a strong contender in the family SUV market.

Citroën Berlingo (18-)

RRP From: £24,205
Pre Reg From: c. £21,000
Used Price From: c. £8,500

Powertrains: Petrol, Diesel, EV
Dimensions: 4403 mm (M) / 4753 mm (XL) × 1848 × 1844 mm
Boot: 775L (1,050L XL)

Key rivals: Dacia Jogger, Peugeot Rifter, Renault Kangoo, Toyota Proace City Verso, Volkswagen Caddy

Few cars offer as much practicality as the Berlingo. Sliding doors, a huge boot and flexible seating make it ideal for growing families, dog owners and outdoor lifestyles. Available with petrol, diesel and electric powertrains, it continues to be one of the most versatile vehicles on sale.

Citroën SpaceTourer (16-)

RRP From: £35,495
Pre Reg From: c. £31,000
Used Price From: c. £8,000

Powertrains: Diesel, EV
Dimensions: 4983 mm (M) / 5333 mm (XL) × 1920 × 1890 mm
Boot: 989L (1,384L XL)

Key rivals: Ford Tourneo Custom, Mercedes-Benz V-Class, Peugeot Traveller, Toyota Proace Verso, Volkswagen Multivan

The SpaceTourer provides genuine MPV practicality, with seating for up to nine people and vast luggage capacity. Whether carrying a large family or used as a shuttle vehicle, it combines comfort with impressive versatility. Diesel and electric versions ensure there is an option to suit a wide range of buyers.
