Land Rover Guide

Land Rover has built its reputation on creating some of the world's most capable luxury SUVs, combining genuine off-road ability with premium comfort and cutting-edge technology. From the rugged Defender to the luxurious Range Rover, every model is designed to tackle challenging terrain while remaining refined and comfortable on the road.

Modern mild hybrid, plug-in hybrid and petrol powertrains ensure the range is as capable on the daily commute as it is on long-distance adventures. Whether you're looking for a practical family SUV, an accomplished towing vehicle or one of the finest luxury cars on sale, Land Rover offers a model to suit.

Current Models

The Discovery Sport combines premium styling with genuine seven-seat versatility in a relatively compact footprint. Comfortable on long journeys and highly capable off-road, it remains an appealing choice for families wanting luxury, practicality and all-weather confidence.

Discovery (17-)

Pre Reg From: c. £59,500
Used Price From: c. £10,500

Dimensions: 4956 × 1990 × 1888 mm
Boot: 996L (5 seats), 258L (7 seats)

Key rivals: Audi Q7, BMW X7, KGM Rexton, Toyota Land Cruiser, Volvo XC90

The Discovery remains one of the most capable and practical large SUVs on sale, but its age leaves it in an awkward position between the Defender 110 and newer Range Rover Sport. Even so, it still offers genuine seven-seat practicality, exceptional towing ability, outstanding off-road capability and an interior few rivals can match.

Range Rover (22-)

RRP From: £107,530
Pre Reg From: c. £95,000
Used Price From: c. £60,000

Powertrains: Diesel, MHEV, PHEV
Dimensions: 5052, 5252 (LWB) × 2005 × 1970 mm
Boot: 818L, 725L LWB, 212L LWB (7 Seats)

Key rivals: BMW X7, Mercedes-Benz GLS, Rolls-Royce Cullinan, Yangwang U8

The Range Rover continues to set the benchmark for luxury SUVs, combining exceptional refinement with genuine off-road capability. A beautifully finished interior, advanced technology and outstanding comfort make it equally suited to chauffeur duties, family transport or cross-country adventures. An optional Long Wheelbase version offers exceptional rear legroom and seven-seat versatility, with its slightly smaller boot reflecting Land Rover's focus on maximising passenger comfort rather than luggage capacity.
