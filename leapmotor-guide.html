Leapmotor Guide

Leapmotor is one of China's fastest-growing electric vehicle manufacturers and is using Stellantis' extensive European dealer network to establish itself quickly across the UK. Its range combines competitive pricing with well-finished interiors, modern technology and durable lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries, helping to keep running costs low while offering long-term peace of mind.

The brand is also among the first to introduce range-extender electric vehicle (REEV) technology to Europe on a larger scale, giving buyers an alternative to conventional hybrids and fully electric cars. With a growing model range, Leapmotor is rapidly becoming a brand worth watching.

Current Models

Leapmotor T03 (25-)

RRP From: £15,995
Pre Reg From: c. £12,000
Used Price From: c. £10,000

Powertrains: Electric
Dimensions: 3620 × 1652 × 1577 mm
Boot: 210L

Key rivals: Dacia Spring, Fiat 500e / 500 Hybrid, Hyundai Inster, Kia Picanto, Toyota Aygo X

The T03 is one of the UK's most affordable electric cars, offering compact dimensions, low running costs and generous standard equipment. Ideal for urban driving, it provides a practical alternative to traditional city cars while making electric motoring accessible to buyers on a tighter budget.

Leapmotor B03X (26-)

RRP From: £28,495
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: N/A

Powertrains: Electric
Dimensions: 4270 × 1810 × 1635 mm
Boot: 510L

Key rivals: BYD Atto 2, Citroën ë-C3 Aircross, Kia EV3, Renault 4, Volvo EX30

The B03X brings Leapmotor's value-focused approach to the compact electric SUV market. A 53kWh LFP battery provides up to 237 miles of WLTP range, while a powerful 194bhp motor and rapid charging give it strong everyday performance. Clever packaging is a major strength, with a spacious cabin, flexible rear seating and an impressive 510-litre boot helping the B03X offer family-car practicality despite its compact dimensions.

Leapmotor B05 (26-)

RRP From: £30,495
Pre Reg From: c. £27,500
Used Price From: c. £27,000

Powertrains: EV
Dimensions: 4430 × 1880 × 1520 mm
Boot: 345L

Key rivals: Kia EV4, MG4, Renault Mégane E-Tech, Vauxhall Astra Electric, Volkswagen ID.3

The B05 takes a different approach to many family EVs, combining rear-wheel drive, near 50:50 weight distribution and a low-slung driving position to create one of the more engaging cars in its class. Rather than simply focusing on technology and value, Leapmotor has also prioritised driving dynamics, giving the B05 a balanced, confidence-inspiring feel on twisting roads, alongside competitive pricing, impressive efficiency and a practical cabin.

Leapmotor B10 (26-)

RRP From: £31,495
Pre Reg From: c. £28,000
Used Price From: c. £23,500

Powertrains: Electric, REEV
Dimensions: 4515 × 1885 × 1655 mm
Boot: 420L (EV), 330L (REEV)

Key rivals: BYD Atto 3 Evo, Hyundai Kona Electric, Kia EV3, Renault Scenic E-Tech, Volkswagen iD.4

The B10 combines competitive pricing with a spacious cabin and the choice of fully electric or range-extender (REEV) powertrains. Its practical dimensions, modern technology and strong value proposition should make it an attractive family SUV for buyers looking beyond the established brands.

Leapmotor C10 (25-)

RRP From: £36,500
Pre Reg From: c. £26,500
