Indcar Mid-Sized Buses & Minibuses

TNY 13 Sep 2026

Indcar Minibuses

Indcar builds a broad range of compact buses and minibuses for school, interurban, tourism and accessible passenger transport. In the UK, its vehicles are represented by Minis to Midis. The Wing focuses on touring, transfers and higher-comfort passenger work, while the Mobi is a more functional Iveco Daily-based model aimed at school, local and interurban services.


Indcar Wing

The Wing is Indcar's higher-spec compact minibus, designed for private hire, tourism, transfers and longer-distance passenger work. Its coach-style body provides more interior and luggage space than a conventional van conversion, while reclining seats, climate control and a large rear luggage compartment give it a more premium feel. The latest generation offers seating for up to 30 passengers.

Price From: POA

Base Chassis: Iveco Daily

Powertrain: Diesel / CNG, specification dependent

Seats: Up to 30

Transmission: Manual / Hi-Matic automatic, specification dependent

Luggage: Large rear luggage compartment with side and rear access

Typical Licence: D

Typical Use: Private hire, tourism, airport transfers, executive transport and longer-distance group travel

Indcar Mobi

The Mobi is Indcar's more functional small-bus range, designed around school, local and interurban transport. Built on the Iveco Daily 70 chassis, it combines relatively compact exterior dimensions with a surprisingly spacious passenger area and can accommodate up to 33 seats. Its simple layout, robust construction and focus on running costs make it particularly suited to operators that prioritise practicality over luxury.

Price From: POA

Base Chassis: Iveco Daily 70

Powertrain: Diesel / CNG, specification dependent

Seats: Up to 33

Transmission: Manual / Hi-Matic automatic, specification dependent

Typical Licence: D

Accessibility: Low-entry and wheelchair-accessible Mobi variants available

Typical Use: School transport, interurban routes, community transport and local passenger services

View Mobi on Indcar →


Indcar e-Mobi City

The e-Mobi City is Indcar's fully electric urban minibus, developed as the zero-emission counterpart to the conventional Mobi City. At around eight metres long, it is aimed at town and city routes where a full-size single-decker would be unnecessarily large. A low-entry layout, wide rear access and standing space make it more like a compact service bus than a conventional passenger-van conversion.

Price From: POA

Powertrain: Electric

Power: 90 kW nominal

Torque: 220 Nm

Passenger Capacity: Up to 30 passengers

Accessibility: Low-entry layout with rear wheelchair access

Typical Licence: D

Typical Use: Urban bus services, town-centre routes, community transport and zero-emission local services
