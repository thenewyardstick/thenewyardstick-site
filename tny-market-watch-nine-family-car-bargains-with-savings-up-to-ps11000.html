TNY Market Watch: Nine Family Car Bargains with Savings Up to £11,000

📈 Market Watch 11 Oct 2026
Chery Tiggo 4 1.5 Hybrid Aspire
Pre-reg price: £17,995
RRP: £19,995
Saving: £2,000

The Chery Tiggo 4 is already remarkably inexpensive at its full list price, making a further £2,000 reduction particularly appealing.

Unlike many affordable crossovers, it features a full-hybrid powertrain, combining a 1.5-litre petrol engine with electric assistance for a quoted 204PS system output. An automatic transmission comes as standard, while Aspire specification includes LED lighting, dual-zone climate control, a reversing camera and twin 12.3-inch displays.

Its compact SUV proportions provide useful family accommodation without making it awkward to park.

Chery remains a relatively new name in Britain, so long-term ownership and resale values are less established. Nevertheless, under £18,000 for a well-equipped full-hybrid automatic SUV represents impressive value.

Citroën C5 Aircross Hybrid 145 Plus
Pre-reg price: £23,998
RRP: £33,670
Saving: £9,672

The new-generation Citroën C5 Aircross is one of the largest and most comfort-focused cars in this selection, making its sub-£24,000 asking price especially interesting.

Its 145PS mild-hybrid petrol powertrain includes an automatic gearbox, while Citroën's Advanced Comfort suspension and supportive seating make it particularly appealing for longer family journeys.

A spacious rear cabin, generous luggage capacity and modern interior technology add to its appeal, with Plus specification offering an impressive level of equipment.

At almost £10,000 below list price, this pre-registered example brings a genuinely substantial, refined family SUV within reach of buyers shopping on a much smaller budget.

Dacia Bigster 1.2 Mild Hybrid Extreme
Pre-reg price: £23,495
RRP: £27,015
Saving: £3,520

Dacia's Bigster takes a more rugged, straightforward approach to family motoring, offering generous interior space and an enormous boot without the premium pricing often associated with larger SUVs.

The 1.2-litre turbocharged petrol engine produces 140PS with mild-hybrid assistance, balancing respectable performance with manageable running costs.

Extreme specification brings an adventurous appearance, 18-inch alloy wheels, modular roof bars and a panoramic opening sunroof, alongside a practical, hard-wearing interior designed for everyday family life.

At £23,495, the Bigster offers plenty of equipment, a substantial luggage compartment and the versatility of a large SUV for considerably less than many similarly sized alternatives. It's an appealing choice for families who prioritise space and everyday usability.

Hyundai Bayon 1.0 T-GDi Black Line
Pre-reg price: £17,000
RRP: £23,795
Saving: £6,795

The Hyundai Bayon combines compact dimensions with crossover styling and a surprisingly useful 411-litre boot, making it an interesting alternative to a conventional supermini.

The Black Line specification is particularly appealing, bringing distinctive dark exterior details, alloy wheels, heated front seats, a heated steering wheel, LED headlights and a 10.25-inch infotainment system.

Its 90PS turbocharged petrol engine is geared towards everyday economy rather than outright performance, but should prove perfectly adequate for commuting and family duties.

At £17,000, this pre-registered Bayon offers an attractive combination of equipment, practicality and manageable running costs.

MG S5 EV SE Standard Range
Pre-reg price: £17,995
RRP: £28,995
Saving: £11,000

Perhaps the biggest surprise here is the MG S5 EV. For less than £18,000, buyers can pick up a nearly new, fully electric family SUV with considerably more space than most similarly priced electric superminis.

The entry-level SE uses a 49kWh battery, offering approximately 211 miles of WLTP range, alongside rear-wheel drive and an automatic transmission. A 453-litre boot and spacious passenger compartment make it a genuinely useful family vehicle.

It won't match the longer-range versions for motorway flexibility, but the combination of modern technology, practical dimensions and an £11,000 saving against list price is difficult to ignore.

For households able to charge at home, this could be an exceptionally economical family car.

Nissan Juke 1.0 DIG-T N-Connecta
Pre-reg price: £15,999
RRP: £25,715
Saving: £9,716

At under £16,000, the Nissan Juke is the cheapest car in our selection, offering an affordable route into a nearly new family crossover.

The 1.0-litre turbocharged petrol engine produces 114bhp, while N-Connecta specification brings a 12.3-inch touchscreen, navigation, a 360-degree camera and 19-inch alloy wheels.

Its 422-litre boot makes it considerably more practical than its compact dimensions might suggest, although rear passenger space isn't as generous as some larger alternatives here.

The manual gearbox won't suit everyone, but with a discount of almost £10,000 against list price, this well-equipped Juke makes a compelling case for itself.

Peugeot 408 Hybrid Allure
Pre-reg price: £20,994
RRP: £32,065 
Saving: £11,071

Few cars in this selection offer quite as much visual presence as the Peugeot 408. Its distinctive fastback styling combines a low, sweeping roofline with raised suspension and a substantial 4.69-metre body.

Despite its unconventional appearance, the 408 offers genuine family practicality, including a generous 536-litre boot in the non-plug-in hybrid version.

The petrol-electric mild-hybrid powertrain includes an automatic gearbox, bringing useful efficiency improvements in town without requiring external charging. Allure trim adds a modern digital cockpit and a comfortable, well-equipped interior.

At barely £21,000, this represents a considerable amount of car for supermini money. The dramatic reduction against the list price makes it one of our most eye-catching finds.

Renault Symbioz 1.3 Mild Hybrid Techno
Pre-reg price: £20,894
RRP: £27,595
Saving: £6,701

Positioned between the Captur and Austral, the Renault Symbioz offers an appealing balance of manageable dimensions and genuine family practicality.

The 1.3-litre mild-hybrid petrol engine produces 140PS, providing stronger performance than many entry-level family crossovers, while official combined fuel economy approaches 48mpg.

A sliding rear bench allows owners to prioritise rear passenger space or luggage capacity, while Techno specification includes a 10.4-inch infotainment touchscreen with Google built-in, digital instruments and adaptive cruise control.

At just under £21,000, the Symbioz becomes a particularly interesting alternative to smaller SUVs, offering generous equipment and flexible accommodation.

Škoda Scala 1.0 TSI SE Edition
Pre-reg price: £16,900
RRP: £24,225
Saving: £7,325 

The Škoda Scala is one of the most overlooked family hatchbacks on sale. At 4.36 metres long, it's virtually identical in length to a Toyota Corolla hatchback, yet this pre-registered example costs less than many new superminis.

A substantial 467-litre boot, generous rear legroom and economical 1.0-litre turbocharged petrol engine make it an excellent everyday family car. SE Edition equipment includes LED headlights, a reversing camera, digital instruments and metallic paint.

Particularly appealing is the availability of both manual and seven-speed DSG automatic versions at £16,900. With no additional outlay for the automatic in these particular deals, the Scala offers remarkable practicality and equipment for the money.

— These nine cars demonstrate just how much value can be found by exploring pre-registered vehicles and discounted new-stock offers. From the affordable Nissan Juke and remarkably spacious Škoda Scala to the fully electric MG S5 and larger family SUVs, there's something here for almost every requirement.

With some models offering savings exceeding £10,000 against list price, buyers could find themselves driving a considerably larger, newer or better-equipped car than their budget might otherwise allow.

Stock and prices can change quickly, but for families prepared to shop around, pre-registration and clearance deals remain excellent ways to find genuine motoring bargains.

This article features a mixture of pre-registered vehicles and discounted new-stock clearance offers. Prices, specifications and advertised savings were correct at the time of publication and are subject to availability and change.
