Peugeot Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Peugeot is the world's oldest surviving automotive brand, tracing its roots back to 1810 when the company was founded as a family steel manufacturer. Having built cars for well over 130 years, Peugeot has grown into one of Europe's best-known manufacturers, producing everything from superminis and family cars to spacious SUVs and versatile people carriers.

Today, stylish design, efficient petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains, plus increasingly premium interiors ensure Peugeot remains one of Europe's strongest mainstream automotive brands. Comfortable ride quality, engaging driving dynamics and competitive pricing continue to make the brand a popular choice with families and company car buyers alike.

Peugeot 208 (20-)

RRP From : £18,995
Pre Reg From : c. £15,000
Used Price From : c. £6,000

Powertrains : Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4055 × 1745 × 1430 mm
Boot : 352L

Key rivals : Alpine A290, MINI Cooper, Renault Clio, Toyota Yaris, Vauxhall Corsa

The 208 combines distinctive styling with one of the most upmarket interiors in the supermini class. Efficient petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains, plus impressive comfort and low running costs, help it remain one of the strongest all-round small cars on the market. The arrival of the 277bhp E-208 GTi also marks the return of Peugeot's iconic hot hatch after an eight-year hiatus.

Peugeot 308 (22-)

RRP From : £29,995
Pre Reg From : c. £19,500
Used Price From : c. £11,000

Powertrains : Diesel, MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4367 × 1852 × 1441 mm
Boot : 412L, 361L PHEV, 608L Estate

Key rivals : BMW 1 Series, Honda Civic, Kia K4, Mazda3, Volkswagen Golf

The 308 brings genuine premium appeal to the family hatchback class with striking styling, a beautifully finished cabin and efficient electrified powertrains. Comfortable, refined and available as both a hatchback and estate, it remains one of Peugeot's most accomplished family cars.

Peugeot 408 (23-)

RRP From : £31,995
Pre Reg From : c. £23,000
Used Price From : c. £14,500

Powertrains : MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4687 × 1848 × 1478 mm
Boot : 536L, 471L PHEV

Key rivals : BYD Sealion 5, Kia K4 Fastback, Mercedes-Benz CLA, Renault Rafale, Toyota C-HR+

The 408 occupies the space between a family hatchback, fastback and SUV, creating one of the most distinctive cars in Peugeot's range. A spacious interior, generous boot and elegant styling combine with efficient electrified powertrains to offer buyers something refreshingly different. Competitive pricing adds to its appeal, with the electric version costing little more than the entry-level mild hybrid while offering around 280 miles of range.

Peugeot 2008 (20-)

RRP From : £24,650
Pre Reg From : c. £17,000
Used Price From : c. £6,500

Powertrains : MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4304 × 1770 × 1550 mm
Boot : 434L

Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Junior, Honda HR-V, Lexus LBX, Renault Captur, Volkswagen Taigo

The 2008 combines bold styling with impressive practicality, offering one of the largest boots in the compact SUV class. Efficient hybrid and electric powertrains, a comfortable ride and generous equipment help it remain one of the strongest all-round small SUVs available.

Peugeot 3008 (24-)

RRP From : £35,035
Pre Reg From : c. £25,000
Used Price From : c. £19,000

Powertrains : MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4542 × 1895 × 1641 mm
Boot : 520L

Key rivals : BMW X2, Honda ZR-V, Jeep Compass, Renault Austral, Toyota C-HR+

The latest 3008 transforms Peugeot's family SUV with bold fastback styling, a striking panoramic interior and an expanded range of hybrid and electric powertrains. Strong practicality, impressive refinement and distinctive design make it one of the most desirable mainstream SUVs on sale.

Peugeot 5008 (24-)

RRP From : £38,650
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £23,000

Powertrains : MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4791 × 1895 × 1694 mm
Boot : 748L, 259L (7 seats)

Key rivals : Chery Tiggo 9, Kia Sorento, Land Rover Discovery Sport, Nissan X-Trail, Volkswagen Tayron

The 5008 combines genuine seven-seat practicality with striking design and a spacious, high-quality interior. Available with hybrid and electric powertrains, it offers excellent versatility for growing families while remaining competitively priced within the seven-seat SUV market, avoiding the bulk of many larger alternatives.

Peugeot Rifter (19-)

RRP From : £29,995
Pre Reg From : c. £29,995
Used Price From : c. £7,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4405, 4755 XL × 1848 × 1818 mm
Boot : 775L, 1050L XL

Key rivals : Citroën Berlingo, Ford Tourneo Courier, Renault Kangoo, Toyota Proace City Verso, Volkswagen Caddy

The Rifter prioritises practicality above all else, offering huge interior space, clever storage solutions and sliding rear doors that make everyday family life easier. Although its van-based design won't appeal to every buyer, those willing to prioritise function over fashion will find one of the most practical family vehicles on sale.

Peugeot Traveller (17-)

RRP From : £43,850
Pre Reg From : c. £37,000
Used Price From : c. £12,000
Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4981, 5331 XL × 1920 × 1890 mm
Boot : 989L, 1,384L XL

Key rivals : Lexus LM, Mercedes-Benz V-Class, Toyota Proace Verso, Vauxhall Vivaro Life, Volkswagen Multivan

The Traveller offers genuine eight- and nine-seat practicality without sacrificing comfort or refinement. A spacious cabin, flexible seating and impressive passenger comfort make it an appealing alternative to traditional MPVs and large SUVs for families, hotels and executive transport operators alike.
