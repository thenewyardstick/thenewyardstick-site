Peugeot Guide

Peugeot is the world's oldest surviving automotive brand, tracing its roots back to 1810 when the company was founded as a family steel manufacturer. Having built cars for well over 130 years, Peugeot has grown into one of Europe's best-known manufacturers, producing everything from superminis and family cars to spacious SUVs and versatile people carriers.

Today, stylish design, efficient petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains, plus increasingly premium interiors ensure Peugeot remains one of Europe's strongest mainstream automotive brands. Comfortable ride quality, engaging driving dynamics and competitive pricing continue to make the brand a popular choice with families and company car buyers alike.

Current Models

Peugeot 208 (20-)

Powertrains : Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4055 × 1745 × 1430 mm

The 208 combines distinctive styling with one of the most upmarket interiors in the supermini class. Efficient petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains, plus impressive comfort and low running costs, help it remain one of the strongest all-round small cars on the market. The arrival of the 281PS E-208 GTi also marks the return of Peugeot's iconic GTi badge after an eight-year hiatus.

The updated 308 brings genuine premium appeal to the family hatchback class with striking styling, a beautifully finished cabin and a broad range of electrified powertrains. Comfortable, refined and available as both a hatchback and estate, it remains one of Peugeot's most accomplished family cars.

The latest update sharpens its design and technology while retaining mild hybrid, plug-in hybrid and fully electric options.
