Morgan Guide

TNY 10 Sep 2026

Morgan has been building distinctive vehicles in Malvern since 1909, and its appeal still rests on making the journey feel like an occasion. Traditional craftsmanship remains central, including hand-shaped body panels and carefully finished interiors, but the current four-wheel cars use bonded aluminium platforms and BMW engines.

UK buyers can order through the factory and a specialist dealer network, with extensive choices of colours, materials and accessories. Super 3 offers a particularly exposed three-wheeled experience; Plus Four is the compact traditional roadster; and Supersport brings a more accommodating approach. These are personal, often highly specified purchases rather than everyday transport for everyone.

Super 3 turns a short drive into an adventure, with an open cockpit, a view of its working front wheels and very little between you and the weather. Its three-wheeled layout and naturally aspirated engine give it a character all its own. It suits an owner seeking a distinctive second vehicle, with touring luggage carried through a choice of optional racks and panniers.

Plus Four mixes Morgan’s familiar narrow-bodied roadster shape with thoroughly modern performance. It is small enough to enjoy on a country lane and sufficiently comfortable to make a longer trip appealing, provided you pack thoughtfully. Choose the manual for greater involvement or the automatic for relaxed progress. It is aimed at buyers who value craftsmanship and character over convenience or fashionable technology.

Supersport makes Morgan ownership a little easier without losing the hand-built, open-road feel. A separate boot, optional removable hardtop and more accommodating cabin make it the natural choice for longer trips. The straight-six engine supplies effortless pace, while the newer 400 version adds a sharper edge. It suits a buyer seeking something more individual than a conventional premium sports car.
