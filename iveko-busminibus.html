IVECO Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

IVECO's Daily range bridges the gap between conventional van-based minibuses and larger purpose-built buses. Its rear-wheel-drive layout, high gross vehicle weights and heavy-duty commercial-vehicle foundations make it particularly well suited to intensive passenger work, while Daily Line, Daily Tourys and electric eDAILY versions cover everything from community and shuttle services to private hire and small-group touring.

IVECO Daily Line

The Daily Line is the more versatile passenger-carrying member of IVECO's minibus range, designed primarily for inter-urban, community and shuttle work. Different lengths, door arrangements and seating configurations allow it to be adapted to a variety of operator requirements, while wheelchair-accessible layouts can also be specified. Underneath, it retains the Daily's rear-wheel-drive architecture and heavy-duty commercial-vehicle foundations, giving it a more substantial feel than many smaller van-derived minibuses.

IVECO Daily Tourys

The Daily Tourys takes the same basic Daily formula in a more coach-like direction, prioritising passenger comfort and luggage capacity for longer-distance work. Reclining seats, dedicated luggage space, air conditioning and overhead storage give it more of a miniature touring-coach character than a conventional van conversion. It is particularly well suited to private hire, tourism, hotel transfers and small-group excursions where comfort and luggage capacity matter as much as outright seating capacity.

IVECO eDAILY Minibus

The eDAILY brings a fully electric drivetrain to IVECO's heavy-duty Daily platform, giving passenger operators a zero-emission alternative without abandoning the flexibility of the conventional model. Its modular battery system allows capacity to be tailored to different routes and payload requirements, while the underlying chassis remains suitable for specialist passenger and accessible conversions. It is particularly suited to urban and regional shuttle, community and private-hire work where predictable daily mileage makes electric operation easier to plan.
