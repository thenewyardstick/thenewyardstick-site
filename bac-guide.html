BAC Guide

BAC stands for Briggs Automotive Company, the Liverpool-based specialist established by brothers Ian and Neill Briggs. It concentrates on a very particular idea: a lightweight, road-legal sports car with one seat and the driver in the centre. The Mono is built in small numbers, with considerable scope to tailor the cockpit and finish to its owner.

This is an enthusiast manufacturer rather than a conventional showroom brand, and UK buyers deal with the company’s specialist operation. The current road car sits alongside a separate track-only Cup programme. Practicality is secondary to the driving experience, so it is normally an additional car.

The Mono turns every drive into a solo event. Its open cockpit, central driving position and minimal bodywork make the connection with motorsport obvious, but this is a road-legal car. The latest updates add more sophisticated driver assistance for different conditions. It is a specialist enthusiast purchase, with weather protection, luggage and carrying a passenger firmly outside the brief.
