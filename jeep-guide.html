Jeep Guide 

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Jeep is one of the world's most recognisable SUV brands, with a heritage stretching back more than 80 years. While famous for its legendary off-road capability, today's UK range also focuses on everyday comfort, efficient hybrid and electric powertrains, and distinctive American-inspired styling. From the compact Avenger to the iconic Wrangler and luxurious Grand Cherokee, every Jeep retains a sense of adventure while offering modern technology and competitive levels of equipment. Whether you're looking for an efficient urban crossover or a capable vehicle for exploring beyond the tarmac, Jeep continues to offer something genuinely different from the mainstream.

Current Models

Jeep Avenger (23-)

RRP From : £26,495
Pre Reg From : c. £16,500
Used Price From : c. £13,500

Powertrains : Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4084 x 1776 x 1528 mm
Boot : 380L, 355L (EV)

Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Junior, Kia EV2, Renault 4, Seat Arona, Toyota Yaris Cross

The Jeep Avenger proves that compact dimensions don't have to mean compromise, packing a surprisingly practical cabin and generous equipment into a footprint that's ideal for town driving. Buyers who regularly carry adults in the rear may prefer the larger Compass, but for most households the Avenger strikes an excellent balance between size, comfort and everyday usability.

Jeep Compass (25-)

RRP From : £35,499
Pre Reg From : c. £28,500
Used Price From : c. £26,500

Powertrains : MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4552 x 1900 x 1675 mm
Boot : 550 litres

Key rivals : Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Leapmotor B10, Renault Austral, Volkswagen Tiguan

The Jeep Compass blends distinctive styling with improved refinement, generous interior space and a choice of efficient hybrid and fully electric powertrains. More spacious than the Avenger yet easier to manoeuvre than larger SUVs, it sits comfortably in the heart of the family crossover market while retaining Jeep's unmistakable character.

Jeep Wrangler (18-)

RRP From : £61,596
Pre Reg From : c. £56,000
Used Price From : c. £26,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4882 x 1894 x 1848 mm
Boot : 548 litres

Key rivals : Ineos Grenadier, Land Rover Defender 110, Mercedes-Benz G-Class, Toyota Land Cruiser

The Wrangler remains one of the world's most capable off-road vehicles, combining iconic styling with genuine all-terrain ability that few rivals can match. Despite its rugged reputation, modern technology and improved refinement make it more usable every day, while its unique character continues to attract buyers wanting something truly authentic.

Jeep Grand Cherokee (22-)

RRP From : £72,414
Pre Reg From : c. £48,500
Used Price From : c. £34,500

Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4914 x 1968 x 1799 mm
Boot : 533 litres

Key rivals : Audi Q7, BMW X5, Range Rover Sport, Lexus RX, Mercedes-Benz GLE

The Grand Cherokee combines impressive comfort, generous interior space and effortless long-distance refinement with genuine off-road capability. Offered exclusively as a plug-in hybrid in the UK, it delivers premium levels of equipment and a distinctive alternative to established luxury SUVs, appealing to buyers who value comfort, capability and individuality.
