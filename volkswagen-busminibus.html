Volkswagen Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Volkswagen's passenger-transport range stretches from the factory-built Transporter Shuttle to larger Crafter-based minibuses produced by specialist converters. The latest Transporter Shuttle is available with diesel, plug-in hybrid and fully electric power, while the Crafter provides the space for higher-capacity and wheelchair-accessible layouts with up to 17 seats.


Volkswagen Transporter Shuttle

The Transporter Shuttle is Volkswagen's factory-built passenger version of the latest Transporter, offering seating for up to nine occupants and a choice of short- or long-wheelbase bodies. Developed alongside the latest Ford Transit Custom and sharing its underlying platform and much of its engineering, it retains Volkswagen-specific styling, equipment and model positioning. Diesel, plug-in hybrid and fully electric versions are available, giving operators a broad choice of powertrains.

Volkswagen Crafter Minibus

The Crafter provides the basis for Volkswagen's larger minibus conversions, with specialist UK converters offering layouts for schools, community transport, private hire and accessible passenger services. Versions with up to 17 seats are available, while tracked floors, passenger lifts and wheelchair spaces allow the interior to be adapted for different operators. Multiple body lengths and engine outputs make the Crafter particularly flexible for specialist passenger conversions.
