The DELIVER 9 is no longer an unfamiliar Chinese newcomer. It has been on UK roads since 2020 and, after several years of fleet sales, has become a relatively common sight. This is a full-size large van with up to 12.9m³ of load space, a 2.0-litre diesel engine and a choice of front- or rear-wheel drive, putting it firmly into Transit, Boxer and Movano territory.

At £19,296 + VAT pre-reg, though, it costs considerably less than most equivalent large vans. For buyers who simply need a lot of usable van for their money, the DELIVER 9 makes a particularly strong value case.

Taken together, these five deals show how aggressively MAXUS is competing on value. The biggest reductions are on the eDELIVER 7 and DELIVER 9, but even the pickups and MIFA 7 are being advertised well below list price.

MAXUS may still lack the badge recognition of some established rivals, but at these pre-reg prices it becomes much harder to dismiss — particularly for buyers more interested in capability, equipment and usable range than the name on the grille.
