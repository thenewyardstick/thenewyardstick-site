Yutong (Pelican) Midibuses & Electric Buses



Yutong E9L

The E9L is Yutong's smallest electric bus currently listed for British operators, combining a relatively compact 9.5-metre body with the passenger facilities of a much larger single-decker. Its 2.42-metre width makes it particularly suitable for rural routes, residential streets and town centres where conventional buses can prove cumbersome. The accessible interior offers 24 fixed seats and two tip-up seats, with additional standing accommodation. A 255.48kWh LFP battery provides energy for daily operations, while rapid DC charging supports depot-based use. Air conditioning, USB charging and Yutong's electric safety systems are included, making the E9L a modern alternative to diesel-powered midibuses. Examples are already operating with British transport companies.

Yutong E10

The E10 moves closer to full-sized single-deck bus territory, combining a 10.9-metre body with seating for up to 33 passengers and a total capacity of 70. It is designed for busy urban and regional services, offering a spacious low-floor interior, accessible passenger accommodation and comprehensive onboard equipment. A substantial 422.87kWh LFP battery provides energy for intensive daily operation, with a smaller battery option also available. Its 215kW electric motor delivers smooth, quiet performance, while air conditioning and modern seating improve passenger comfort. Already established among British bus operators, the E10 provides a versatile zero-tailpipe-emission alternative to conventional diesel single-deckers, particularly where passenger demand doesn't justify a larger vehicle.

Important Information

Prices, specifications, dimensions, seating capacities, weights, power outputs and equipment are based primarily on information published by vehicle manufacturers, their official UK representatives and established specialist suppliers. Specifications can vary by trim, configuration, model year and individual vehicle.

Prices shown are UK prices where available and are intended as a guide. Where an official UK list price is unavailable, pricing may be shown as approximate or POA. Prices can change and may exclude optional equipment, delivery charges or other operator-specific costs.

Passenger seating capacities must not be confused with total passenger capacities. Both Yutong models can accommodate standing passengers, while wheelchair positions, tip-up seats and interior layouts affect the number of available seats. The E9L's maximum total capacity is quoted differently across official UK distributor materials.

Licence categories shown are a general guide only. Individual driving entitlement can vary according to passenger-seat count, vehicle weight, vehicle length, licence issue date, grandfather rights, Section 19 operation and other exemptions. Passenger-seat counts used for licence purposes exclude the driver. Always check the vehicle's plated specification and your own driving-licence entitlement before driving.

Electric driving range depends on passenger load, route characteristics, operating speed, weather, heating and air conditioning. Battery capacities and maximum charging rates are manufacturer figures and can vary by specification. Quoted maximum charging power is not maintained throughout the entire charging process.

Specifications are checked against the latest information reasonably available to The New Yardstick at the time of publication or update. Manufacturers and distributors may alter specifications, pricing or model availability without notice.
