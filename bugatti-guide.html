Bugatti Guide

Bugatti builds exceptionally exclusive performance cars in Molsheim, France, continuing a marque established by Ettore Bugatti in 1909. Its modern identity combines extraordinary engineering with lavish craftsmanship and extensive personalisation.

The brand is moving from its long-running W16 engine to the Tourbillon’s V16 hybrid powertrain. Its catalogue also contains historic models and bespoke one-offs, so a model appearing on a dealer website does not automatically mean new production remains available.

Tourbillon pairs an enormous naturally aspirated engine with electric assistance, but the attraction goes well beyond acceleration. Its intricate analogue instruments, dramatic doors and carefully finished cabin make it as much a collector’s object as a driving machine. This is for an exceptionally small audience seeking craftsmanship and engineering theatre, with allocation, bespoke specification and specialist support central to the purchase.
