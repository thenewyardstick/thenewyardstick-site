KGM Motors Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

KGM Motors, formerly known as SsangYong, combines decades of SUV and pick-up experience with a renewed focus on value, practicality and modern technology. The rebrand marks a fresh chapter for the South Korean manufacturer, whose range continues to prioritise generous equipment, spacious interiors and impressive towing ability. While KGM may not enjoy the same recognition as some mainstream rivals, its vehicles often represent excellent value for money, particularly for buyers seeking capable family SUVs or dependable workhorses. From the compact Tivoli to the rugged Musso pick-up, KGM offers honest, practical motoring with a distinctive character.

Current Models

KGM Tivoli (15-)

RRP From : £24,045
Pre Reg From : c. £24,000
Used Price From : c. £2,500

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4225 × 1810 × 1621 mm
Boot : 427L

Key rivals : Dacia Duster, Hyundai Bayon, Nissan Juke, Renault Captur, Volkswagen T-Cross

The Tivoli remains one of the most affordable compact SUVs on sale, offering generous standard equipment, a comfortable driving experience and compact dimensions that make it well suited to town and city driving. It represents excellent value for buyers wanting a practical crossover without stretching the budget.

KGM Torres (24-)

RRP From : £35,995
Pre Reg From : c. £27,000
Used Price From : c. £19,000

Powertrains : Hybrid, EV
Dimensions : 4700 × 1890 × 1720 mm
Boot : 668L, 703L (EV)

Key rivals : Leapmotor C10, MG HS, Mitsubishi Outlander, Nissan X-Trail

The Torres stands out with bold styling, a spacious interior and generous levels of standard equipment. Available with Hybrid or fully electric power, it offers family-friendly practicality and a comfortable driving experience, making it an appealing alternative to more familiar mainstream SUVs.

KGM Actyon (25-)

RRP From : £38,995
Pre Reg From : c. £32,000
Used Price From : c. £23,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4740 × 1910 × 1680 mm
Boot : 668L

Key rivals : BYD Seal U, Omoda 9, Peugeot 408, Renault Rafale

The Actyon brings a more stylish, coupe-inspired design to the KGM range without sacrificing the practicality expected from a family SUV. A spacious cabin, generous equipment and comfortable ride quality make it a distinctive choice for buyers wanting something a little different.

KGM Rexton (17-)

RRP From : £42,615
Pre Reg From : c. £37,000
Used Price From : c. £8,000

Powertrains : Diesel
Dimensions : 4850 × 1960 × 1825 mm
Boot : 240L (7 seats), 641L (5 seats)

Key rivals : Ineos Grenadier, Jeep Grand Cherokee, Land Rover Discovery, Toyota Land Cruiser

The Rexton is a traditional body-on-frame SUV built for buyers who need serious towing capability, seven-seat practicality and genuine off-road ability. Strong value, generous equipment and impressive long-distance comfort make it a compelling alternative to far more expensive large SUVs.

KGM Musso (18-)

RRP From : £39,576
Pre Reg From : c. £26,500
Used Price From : c. £8,000

Powertrains : Diesel, EV
Dimensions : 5095 mm (Musso), 5405 mm (Musso Rhino) × 1950 × 1870 mm
Boot : Pick-up load bed

Key rivals : Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max, Maxus T90 EV, Toyota Hilux

The Musso combines impressive payload and towing capabilities with one of the most comfortable cabins in the pick-up market. While the vehicle pictured is the new all-electric Musso EV, diesel versions remain available alongside it, giving buyers a choice of powertrains. With generous equipment, a choice of standard or Rhino load bed lengths and strong value for money, the Musso range deserves to be on every pick-up shortlist.
