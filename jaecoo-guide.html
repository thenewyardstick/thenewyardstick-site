Jaecoo Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Jaecoo is one of the newest names to arrive in the UK, but it's backed by Chery, one of China's largest and fastest-growing automotive manufacturers. Positioned slightly more upmarket than sister brand Omoda, Jaecoo focuses on rugged-looking SUVs that combine generous equipment, modern technology and competitive pricing.

Early reviews from some traditional motoring publications were mixed, yet strong UK sales and generally positive owner feedback have encouraged a broader re-evaluation of the brand's strengths. Buyers can now choose from petrol, plug-in hybrid and fully electric models, with comfort, practicality and value taking priority over outright performance. For those willing to look beyond the badge, Jaecoo is quickly establishing itself as one of the UK's most successful automotive newcomers.

Current Models

Jaecoo 5 (25-)

RRP From : £24,555
Pre Reg From : c. £21,500
Used Price From : c. £21,000


Powertrains : Petrol, EV
Dimensions : 4380 × 1860 × 1650 mm
Boot : 480L, 410L (EV)


Key rivals : Dacia Duster, Hyundai Kona, Kia EV3,  Suzuki S-Cross, Vauxhall Frontera

The Jaecoo 5 brings bold SUV styling into the compact crossover class, offering generous equipment, a modern interior and excellent value for money. It's designed with comfort and practicality in mind rather than sporty handling, making it an appealing choice for buyers wanting a well-equipped family SUV without stretching the budget.

Jaecoo 7 (25-)

RRP From : £29,210
Pre Reg From : c. £26,500
Used Price From : c. £22,500


Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid, PHEV
Dimensions : 4500 × 1865 × 1680 mm
Boot : 500L, 410L (PHEV)


Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Tonale, Audi Q3, Kia Sportage, Hyundai Tucson, MG HS

The Jaecoo 7 combines striking looks with a spacious cabin, impressive technology and the choice of efficient petrol or plug-in hybrid powertrains. Comfortable, refined and competitively priced, it offers a strong alternative to established family SUVs, while generous standard equipment helps it stand out in an increasingly crowded market.

Jaecoo 8 (26-)

RRP From : £45,500
Pre Reg From : c. £42,000
Used Price From : c. £41,000


Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4820 × 1930 × 1710 mm
Boot : 738L (5 seats), 110L (7 seats)

Key rivals : Hyundai Santa Fe, Kia Sorento, Mazda CX-80, Peugeot 5008, Volkswagen Tayron

The Jaecoo 8 is the brand's flagship SUV, offering seven-seat practicality, a premium-feeling interior and generous levels of standard equipment. It delivers impressive comfort and technology for the money, although value-focused buyers should also compare the closely related Chery Tiggo 9, which shares much of its engineering while undercutting the Jaecoo 8 on price.
