TEMSA Midibuses & Mid-Sized Coaches



TEMSA MD9 

The MD9 is TEMSA's established British-market midicoach, offering much of the comfort and equipment of a full-sized touring coach within a shorter 9.5-metre body. Its powerful six-cylinder Cummins diesel engine, automatic transmission and generous luggage capacity make it particularly suitable for private hire, tourism, school contracts and longer-distance passenger transport. Seating arrangements extend to 39 passengers, although 34-seat executive and wheelchair-accessible configurations are also available. PSVAR-compliant versions provide integrated wheelchair access, while independent front suspension and rear air suspension promise a comfortable journey. For operators requiring substantial passenger capacity without the dimensions of a full-sized coach, the MD9 offers an appealing compromise.
