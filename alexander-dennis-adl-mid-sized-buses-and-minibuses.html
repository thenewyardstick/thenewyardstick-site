Alexander Dennis (ADL) Mid-Sized Buses & Minibuses

TNY 13 Sep 2026

Alexander Dennis Buses

Alexander Dennis is one of Britain's best-known bus manufacturers, with the Enviro range covering everything from compact rural and urban buses to full-size single- and double-deck models. Its smaller single-deck range currently combines the compact battery-electric Enviro100EV, the established diesel Enviro200 and the new-generation electric Enviro200EV, giving operators a choice of vehicle sizes and powertrains for local, urban, rural and shuttle services.


Alexander Dennis Enviro100EV

The Enviro100EV is Alexander Dennis's compact battery-electric bus, designed to provide the accessibility and passenger environment of a larger service bus in a much smaller footprint. At just 8.5 metres long and 2.35 metres wide, it is particularly well suited to town centres, residential routes, rural services and other roads where a conventional full-size bus can be cumbersome. Despite its compact dimensions, it can accommodate up to 45 passengers in total, including up to 26 seated passengers.

Alexander Dennis Enviro200

The Enviro200 is Alexander Dennis's established diesel single-deck bus and remains available alongside the manufacturer's newer electric models. A particularly broad choice of body lengths allows it to cover everything from relatively quiet rural and local routes to higher-capacity urban services. Its lightweight construction is designed around fuel efficiency and low operating costs, while wheelchair-accessible low-floor layouts and optional luggage equipment also allow it to serve airport and shuttle work.

Alexander Dennis Enviro200EV

The new-generation Enviro200EV brings Alexander Dennis's long-running single-deck formula into the electric era. Available in several lengths, it combines a 400 kWh battery with a lightweight body designed to reduce energy consumption and maximise passenger capacity. Depending on length, it can provide up to 43 seats and carry as many as 92 passengers in total, making it suitable for substantially busier routes than the smaller Enviro100EV.
