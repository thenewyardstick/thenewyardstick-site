Czinger Guide

Czinger is a young American hypercar manufacturer based in Los Angeles. Established in 2019, it combines a bespoke hybrid powertrain with computer-assisted design and metal additive manufacturing, using unusual production methods to create complex structural components. Its first road car, the 21C, places the passenger behind the driver rather than alongside.

H.R. Owen represents the brand in Britain and announced its first UK customer handover in July 2025. This is a tiny, specialist market where specification, allocation and factory support matter more than headline discounts. Buyers should obtain an individual UK quotation and confirm delivery timing directly with the retailer.

The 21C is for someone who wants their hypercar to feel different before it even moves. Its central driving position and tandem passenger seat give it a cockpit-like atmosphere, while the hybrid system and extensive aerodynamic work serve serious performance ambitions. HDF favours downforce; VMax takes a more streamlined approach. Ownership is an individual factory-and-retailer relationship, with little conventional used-market price evidence.
