Citroën Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Citroën's UK passenger-carrying range spans the nine-seat SpaceTourer and fully electric ë-SpaceTourer, while specialist converters also offer larger Relay-based minibuses for schools, charities, care providers and community transport operators.

Citroën SpaceTourer

The diesel SpaceTourer sits at the car-like end of Citroën's passenger range, combining van-derived interior space with up to nine seats. It suits large families, hotels, private-hire operators and organisations needing considerably more passenger capacity than a conventional MPV without moving into a full-size minibus.

RRP From: £42,275 OTR

Powertrain: 2.0 Diesel, 180 hp, 8-speed automatic

Seats: Up to 9

Typical Licence: B

Body Sizes: M / XL

Length: c.4,981 mm (M) / c.5,331 mm (XL)

Width: c.1,920 mm excluding mirrors

Height: c.1,890 mm

Fuel Consumption: Specification dependent

Citroën ë-SpaceTourer

The ë-SpaceTourer uses the same versatile passenger-focused body as the diesel model but replaces the combustion engine with a 136 hp electric drivetrain. With up to nine seats and a WLTP range of more than 200 miles, it is particularly suited to family, hotel, shuttle and urban passenger work where regular charging is available.

RRP From: £36,995 OTR

Powertrain: Electric, 136 hp

Battery: 75 kWh

Range: Up to 216 miles WLTP

Energy Consumption: c.242 Wh/km, specification dependent

Charging: Up to 100 kW DC

Rapid Charging: c.20–80% in 36 minutes

Seats: Up to 9

Typical Licence: B

Body Sizes: M / XL

Length: c.4,981 mm (M) / c.5,331 mm (XL)

Width: c.1,920 mm excluding mirrors

Height: c.1,890 mm

Citroën Relay Minibus

Relay-based minibuses are produced by specialist converters rather than forming one fixed factory Citroën specification. UK conversions commonly offer up to 17 seats and can be configured for schools, community transport, care providers and wheelchair-accessible work. Equipment, seating layout, weight and accessibility therefore vary considerably between conversions.

Price From: POA / conversion dependent

Base Vehicle: Citroën Relay

Powertrain: Diesel

Typical Engine: 2.2 BlueHDi, conversion dependent

Seats: Up to 17

Typical Licence: D1 (B possible in certain circumstances — see Important Information)

Typical Body: L4H2

Length: c.6,363 mm for current L4 Relay base vehicle

Width: c.2,050 mm excluding mirrors

Height: c.2,524 mm for L4H2 base vehicle

Accessibility: Conversion dependent; wheelchair-accessible layouts are widely available

Citroën ë-Relay Minibus

The ë-Relay provides a fully electric version of Citroën's large-van platform and can be used by specialist converters as the basis for zero-emission minibus, shuttle and accessible passenger-transport applications. Current UK ë-Relay models use a large 110 kWh battery and are available in conversion-friendly body styles, although final seating capacity, weight and accessibility equipment depend on the individual conversion.
