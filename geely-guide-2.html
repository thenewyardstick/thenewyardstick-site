Geely Guide

TNY 9 Sep 2026

Geely is a Chinese carmaker whose wider automotive group also has interests in familiar names including Volvo, Polestar and Lotus. Its own-brand UK passenger-car business began in 2025 with the electric EX5, followed by a smaller electric hatchback and a plug-in hybrid SUV in 2026.

The range targets mainstream buyers with competitive pricing, substantial equipment and an expanding dealer network. British engineering connections form part of the story, but these are Geely models with their own identities. The challenge now is turning that strong industrial backing into a recognisable standalone brand for UK buyers.

Current Models

EX2 (26-)

Photo: Geely

RRP From: £20,990
Pre Reg From: c. £20,500
Used Price From: c. £20,500

Powertrains: EV
Dimensions: 4135 × 1805 × 1580 mm
Boot: 375L

Key rivals: Renault 5, Citroën ë-C3, BYD Dolphin Surf, Hyundai Inster, Fiat Grande Panda

The EX2 is Geely’s small-car entry point, combining compact dimensions with considerably more interior space than a traditional city car. It is particularly relevant to commuters and small families who want an affordable EV without moving into a bulky SUV. The keen starting price helps, while its rear-wheel-drive layout and additional front storage give it a few useful distinctions of its own.

EX5 (25-)

Photo: Geely

RRP From: £31,990
Pre Reg From: c. £24,000
Used Price From: c. £21,500

Powertrains: EV
Dimensions: 4615 × 1901 × 1670 mm
Boot: 461L

Key rivals: Aion V, Citroën ë-C5 Aircross, Kia EV5, MG S5 EV, Renault Scenic E-Tech

The EX5 puts comfort, cabin space and a substantial equipment list ahead of sporty styling. It is an easy model to shortlist if the family needs a sensible electric SUV and wants to try a newer brand. Its combination of generous space, competitive pricing and everyday refinement gives Geely a credible entry point into the busy family-EV market.

Starray EM-i (26–)

Photo: Geely

RRP From: £29,990
Pre Reg From: c. £25,000
Used Price From: c. £23,000

Powertrains: PHEV
Dimensions: 4740 × 1905 × 1685 mm
Boot: 528L

Key rivals: BYD Seal U DM-i, Chery Tiggo 7, Jaecoo 8, Leapmotor C10 REEV, MG HS PHEV

The Starray EM-i is aimed at families who can charge at home but still want petrol flexibility for longer trips. It offers the familiar raised driving position and roomy interior of a family SUV, with a strong emphasis on value. Its combination of plug-in hybrid efficiency, generous space and competitive pricing gives Geely a broader family-car offering beyond its fully electric models.
