Mercedes-Benz Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Mercedes-Benz covers a broad part of the passenger-transport market, from nine-seat Vito Tourer models to larger Sprinter-based minibuses. Diesel and fully electric versions provide options for shuttle, taxi, private-hire and community transport, while the larger Sprinter platform can be adapted by specialist converters for higher-capacity and wheelchair-accessible passenger layouts.

Mercedes-Benz Vito Tourer

The Vito Tourer is the passenger-carrying version of Mercedes-Benz's mid-sized van, aimed at operators who need more seats than a conventional MPV without moving into a full-size minibus. With space for up to nine occupants, sliding side doors and a car-like driving environment, it is particularly well suited to airport transfers, hotel shuttles, private hire and larger-family transport. Different body lengths and seating arrangements allow the cabin to be tailored to passenger and luggage requirements.

Mercedes-Benz eVito Tourer

The eVito Tourer applies a fully electric drivetrain to the same basic passenger-van format, retaining up to nine-seat practicality while removing local tailpipe emissions. Its quieter drivetrain and smooth low-speed operation suit airport, hotel, taxi and urban shuttle work particularly well, especially where routes are predictable and charging can be planned around regular stops. Passenger space remains the main priority, with sliding side doors and flexible seating arrangements helping it work as a practical electric shuttle.

Mercedes-Benz Sprinter Minibus

The Sprinter is one of the most widely used foundations for larger minibuses in the UK, with specialist converters offering a broad range of passenger layouts for schools, community transport, care organisations, private hire and accessible transport. Its long body options, high roof and strong commercial-vehicle platform allow considerably more flexibility than a smaller passenger van, while seating, luggage space and wheelchair provision can all be tailored to the operator's requirements.

Mercedes-Benz eSprinter Minibus

The eSprinter provides an electric foundation for larger specialist passenger conversions, combining the space and flexibility of the Sprinter with a fully electric drivetrain. Passenger layouts can be tailored by specialist converters for shuttle, community and accessible transport, while two battery sizes allow operators to balance range, payload and cost. It is particularly suited to predictable passenger routes where vehicles can return to a depot or other regular charging location between duties.
