MINI Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

MINI has evolved from a characterful small hatchback into a premium range of stylish hatchbacks, crossovers and electric vehicles. While unmistakable design remains at the heart of every model, today's line-up also offers improved technology, greater versatility and a choice of petrol or electric power.

The compact Cooper continues to define the brand, while the Aceman and Countryman broaden MINI's appeal for families seeking extra space. Strong residual values, distinctive styling and an engaging driving experience continue to set MINI apart, making it one of the UK's most recognisable premium small car manufacturers.

Current Models

MINI Cooper 3-door (24-)

RRP From : £24,995
Pre Reg From : c. £21,000
Used Price From : c. £15,500

Powertrains : Petrol, EV
Dimensions : 3,876 × 1,744 × 1,452 mm
Boot : 210 litres

Key rivals : Fiat 500e / Grande Panda, Hyundai Inster, Renault 5, Toyota Aygo X

The latest MINI Cooper retains the iconic styling and go-kart handling that have defined the brand for decades, while offering improved technology, refinement and a choice of petrol or electric power. Although practicality remains limited, its premium interior, distinctive design and enjoyable driving experience continue to make it one of the UK's most desirable small cars.

MINI Cooper 5-door (24-)

RRP From : £25,995
Pre Reg From : c. £22,000
Used Price From : c. £18,500

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4,036 × 1,744 × 1,464 mm
Boot : 275 litres

Key rivals : Audi A1 Sportback, Jeep Avenger, Mazda 2 Hybrid, Peugeot 208, Renault Clio

The Cooper 5-door adds a little extra practicality without losing the character that makes a MINI so appealing. Rear passenger space and boot capacity improve over the three-door model, making it a better everyday choice while retaining premium quality, distinctive styling and an enjoyable driving experience.

MINI Cooper Convertible (24-)

RRP From : £31,500
Pre Reg From : c. £28,000
Used Price From : c. £25,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 3,879 × 1,744 × 1,431 mm
Boot : 160 litres

Key rivals : Abarth 500e Convertible, Mazda MX-5, MG Cyberster, Volkswagen T-Roc Cabriolet

The MINI Convertible combines open-top driving with the brand's unmistakable character and premium cabin. It remains one of the few affordable convertibles still available in the UK, offering a fun driving experience and distinctive styling for buyers wanting something a little different.

MINI Aceman (24-)

RRP From : £31,220
Pre Reg From : c. £26,500
Used Price From : c. £23,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4,079 × 1,754 × 1,514 mm
Boot : 300 litres

Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Junior, Fiat 600e, Jeep Avenger, Kia EV2, Renault 4 E-Tech

The Aceman fills the gap between the Cooper and Countryman, bringing MINI styling to the fast-growing compact electric crossover segment. Its distinctive design, premium interior and competitive electric range help it stand out, although several similarly priced rivals offer more passenger and boot space.

MINI Countryman (24-)

RRP From : £31,440
Pre Reg From : c. £28,000
Used Price From : c. £24,000

Powertrains : Petrol, Diesel, EV
Dimensions : 4,444 × 1,843 × 1,661 mm
Boot : 505 litres

Key rivals : BMW X1, BYD Atto 3 EVO, Leapmotor B10, Renault Symbioz, Volvo XC40

The latest Countryman is the biggest and most practical MINI ever produced, offering generous passenger space, a 505-litre boot and a choice of petrol, diesel and electric power. Improved refinement and technology help justify its move further upmarket, while its distinctive styling ensures it still stands out from conventional family SUVs.
