Mercedes-Benz Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Mercedes-Benz has shaped the automotive industry for well over a century, tracing its origins back to Karl Benz's invention of the world's first motor car in 1886. Today, its vast range spans everything from compact hatchbacks and luxury limousines to high-performance AMG models, electric vehicles, vans and the iconic Unimog.

Few manufacturers offer such breadth while maintaining a reputation for engineering, comfort and technological innovation. Whether you're looking for an affordable family car, an executive saloon, a luxury SUV or a capable commercial vehicle, Mercedes-Benz continues to set benchmarks across almost every segment of the automotive market.
