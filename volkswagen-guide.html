Volkswagen Guide

Volkswagen continues to offer one of the broadest ranges in the mainstream market, covering compact hatchbacks, family cars, SUVs, electric models, people carriers and pick-ups.

The range is also undergoing another major transition. The new ID. Polo brings electric power into the supermini class, while the redesigned ID.3 Neo moves Volkswagen's dedicated electric hatchback into a new generation. Petrol, mild-hybrid, plug-in hybrid and diesel models remain important elsewhere, ensuring buyers still have an unusually broad choice of powertrains.

Current Models

Volkswagen Polo (17-)

RRP From : £22,055
Pre Reg From : c. £18,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4074 × 1751 × 1451 mm
Boot : 351L

The Polo continues to offer a mature, comfortable driving experience with strong refinement and a spacious interior. Upcoming mild hybrid technology should reduce fuel consumption and emissions, helping the Polo remain competitive against newer rivals while preserving the comfort and practicality buyers expect.

Volkswagen ID. Polo (26-)

RRP From : £22,445
Pre Reg From : N/A
Used Price From : N/A

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4053 × 1816 × 1530 mm
Boot : 441L

Key rivals : BYD Dolphin, Fiat Grande Panda, Hyundai Ioniq 3, Peugeot E-208, Renault 5 E-Tech 

The ID. Polo brings one of Volkswagen's most familiar names into the electric era without replacing the conventional Polo. Its front-wheel-drive MEB+ platform provides efficient packaging, with a 441L boot that is larger than the Golf's despite compact exterior dimensions. Two battery sizes provide up to 281 miles of WLTP range, while the cabin has familiar controls and returns to physical buttons.

Volkswagen T-Cross (19-)

RRP From : £25,985
Pre Reg From : c. £19,500
Used Price From : c. £7,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4135 × 1760 × 1573 mm
Boot : 455L

Key rivals : Citroën C3 Aircross, Jeep Avenger, Renault Captur, SEAT Arona, Toyota Yaris Cross

The T-Cross is Volkswagen’s most practical and value-led small SUV, combining a compact footprint with impressive interior space. Solid driving dynamics and excellent everyday usability make it an ideal small SUV for smaller families, while its practicality helps keep it competitive in an increasingly crowded segment.

Volkswagen T-Roc (25-)

Powertrains : MHEV
Dimensions : 4372 × 1828 × 1573 mm
Boot : 475L

The new T-Roc moves upmarket with a more technology-led cabin centred around a large 12.9-inch touchscreen and a simplified model range. Increased dimensions provide more passenger space and a larger boot, while mild-hybrid powertrains and improved refinement make Volkswagen’s popular compact SUV a more complete and grown-up family car.

Volkswagen Golf (20-)

RRP From : £29,040
Pre Reg From : c. £22,000
Used Price From : c. £8,000

Dimensions : 4289 × 1789 × 1491 mm
Boot : 381L

Key rivals : BMW 1-Series, Kia EV4, Peugeot 308, Toyota Corolla, Vauxhall Astra

The Golf offers a wide choice of petrol, diesel and long-range plug-in hybrid powertrains alongside a high-tech cabin centred around a large touchscreen. Comfortable, refined and easy to drive, it remains a strong all-rounder, although its 381-litre boot is now smaller than many family hatchback rivals.

Dimensions : 4265 × 1809 × 1561 mm
Boot : 383L

Key rivals : Citroën ë-C4, Kia EV4, Leapmotor B05, MG4, Peugeot E-308

The ID.3 remains a well-balanced family EV, offering good efficiency, comfortable ride quality and a compact footprint that's easy to live with. While newer rivals now offer more space for similar money, the Volkswagen continues to impress with its refinement, technology and mature driving manners. Recent facelift carries ID.3 Neo nameplate.

Volkswagen Tiguan (24-)

RRP From : £38,920
Pre Reg From : c. £29,000
Used Price From : c. £22,500

Powertrains : MHEV, Diesel, PHEV
Dimensions : 4539 × 1842 × 1658 mm
Boot : 652L, 490L PHEV

Key rivals : Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Peugeot 3008, Renault Austral, Toyota RAV4

The Tiguan is a practical family SUV with plenty of passenger space, a useful boot and a technology-focused interior centred around a large touchscreen. A broad choice of petrol, diesel and plug-in hybrid powertrains adds to its versatility, although prices have climbed and higher-spec versions can become expensive. It remains a well-rounded choice for everyday family use.

Volkswagen ID.4 (21-)

RRP From : £35,495
Pre Reg From : c. £26,500
Used Price From : c. £9,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4584 × 1852 × 1634 mm
Boot : 543L

Key rivals : Citroën ë-C5 Aircross, Jeep Compass, Leapmotor B10, Nissan Ariya, Renault Scenic E-Tech

The ID.4 remains Volkswagen's mainstream electric family SUV, combining a spacious cabin with useful range and strong everyday practicality. Newer rivals have increased the pressure on it, but competitive pricing, substantial pre-registered discounts and increasingly affordable used examples continue to make it appealing. It also provides an important bridge in Volkswagen's range until its next generation of electric SUVs arrives.

Volkswagen ID.5 (22-)

RRP From : £35,495
Pre Reg From : c. £29,500
Used Price From : c. £14,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4589 × 1852 × 1615 mm
Boot : 549L

Key rivals : Ford Capri, Hyundai Ioniq 5, Nissan Ariya, Peugeot E-3008, Toyota bZ4X

The ID.5 takes the ID.4 formula in a more style-led direction, adding a coupé-SUV roofline without sacrificing much of the practicality underneath. Its 549L boot is actually slightly larger than the ID.4's, while similar electric hardware keeps range and performance competitive. It remains a useful part of Volkswagen's electric range while the brand transitions towards its next generation of SUVs.

Volkswagen Tayron (25-)

RRP From : £41,635
Pre Reg From : c. £34,500
Used Price From : c. £28,000

Powertrains : MHEV, Petrol, Diesel, PHEV
Dimensions : 4792 × 1853 × 1666 mm
Boot : 652L

Key rivals : Chery Tiggo 9, Hyundai Santa Fe, Jaecoo 8, Mazda CX-80, Peugeot 5008

The Tayron replaces the old Tiguan Allspace and provides a straightforward step up from the regular Tiguan. Five- and seven-seat layouts are available alongside petrol, diesel and plug-in hybrid powertrains, while the longer body provides substantially more passenger space. It is aimed squarely at families needing genuine three-row versatility without moving into a much larger luxury SUV.

Volkswagen Passat (24-)

RRP From : £40,875
Pre Reg From : c. £31,500
Used Price From : c. £22,000

Powertrains : MHEV, PHEV
Dimensions : 4917 × 1849 × 1506 mm
Boot : 690L, 510L PHEV

Key rivals : BYD Seal 6, Jaecoo 8, MG S9, Peugeot 5008, Škoda Superb

The Passat is a refreshingly traditional alternative to the increasingly SUV-heavy large-car market. Closely related to the Škoda Superb, it offers excellent passenger space, a large boot and strong long-distance comfort. Those seeking maximum versatility and space may still prefer a large SUV, but for families who don’t want one, the Passat remains a practical choice.

Volkswagen ID.7 (23-)

RRP From : £51,445
Pre Reg From : c. £35,000
Used Price From : c. £21,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4961 × 1862 × 1536 mm
Boot : 532L, 605L Tourer

Key rivals : BYD Seal, DS No8, Hyundai Ioniq 6, Mazda 6e, Volvo ES90

The ID.7 is Volkswagen's electric alternative to the Passat, combining excellent refinement with generous passenger space and impressive long-distance efficiency. Buyers can choose between the fastback and even more practical Tourer estate, while the largest battery provides more than 400 miles of WLTP range. Heavy early depreciation also makes used examples considerably more attractive than their relatively high list prices suggest.

Volkswagen Caddy (20-)

RRP From : £37,171
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £15,000

Powertrains : Petrol, Diesel
Dimensions : 4500 / 4853 × 1855 × 1833 mm
Boot : 1213L (5-seat), 1720L (Maxi), 119L (7-seat), 446L (7-seat Maxi)

Key rivals : Ford Tourneo Connect, Mercedes-Benz Citan Tourer, Peugeot Rifter, Renault Kangoo, Toyota Proace City Verso

The Caddy shares much of its engineering with the Ford Transit Connect, helping Volkswagen reduce development costs while retaining its own character. Van-based origins provide huge amounts of passenger and luggage space, making it a particularly practical family car. Inside, considerably more technology than previous generations gives the latest Caddy a more modern, car-like feel.

Transporter Shuttle (25-)

RRP From : £49,735
Pre Reg From : c. £44,000
Used Price From : c. £32,000

Powertrains : Diesel, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 5050 × 2032 × 1980 mm (SWB)
Boot: 672L, 819L (LWB)

Key rivals : Ford Tourneo Custom, Kia PV5, Maxus Mifa 9, Toyota Proace Verso, Vauxhall Vivaro Life

The Transporter Shuttle is Volkswagen’s large nine-seat passenger carrier, sharing its underpinnings with the Ford Transit Custom. Short- and long-wheelbase versions are offered with diesel, plug-in hybrid and electric power, while removable rear seating gives it huge flexibility for passengers or luggage. It is particularly well suited to large families, hotels, airport transfers and other passenger-carrying work.

Volkswagen ID. Buzz (22-)

RRP From : £60,005
Pre Reg From : c. £48,000
Used Price From : c. £26,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4712 / 4962 × 1985 × 1927 mm
Boot : 1121L (SWB), 1340L (LWB), 306L (LWB 7-seat)

Key rivals : Ford Tourneo Custom, Kia PV5, Peugeot E-Traveller, Toyota Proace Verso Electric, Vauxhall Vivaro Life Electric

The ID. Buzz takes a more style-led approach than most large electric people carriers, combining distinctive retro-inspired design with genuinely useful interior space. Short- and long-wheelbase versions are available, with the latter providing up to seven seats, while substantial depreciation has made used examples considerably more accessible than the high new-car price suggests.

Volkswagen Amarok (23-)

RRP From : £48,140 + VAT
Pre Reg From : c. £45,000
Used Price From : c. £24,000

Powertrains : Diesel
Dimensions : 5350 × 1910 × 1888 mm
Boot : Pickup

Key rivals : Ford Ranger, Isuzu D-Max, KGM Musso, Maxus T90 EV, Toyota Hilux

The Amarok is closely related to the Ford Ranger, combining proven pick-up foundations with Volkswagen’s own styling and interior treatment. Buyers wanting a plug-in hybrid will need to choose the Ford for now, while a growing number of new rivals are increasing competition. Even so, the Amarok remains a strong workhorse, with substantial towing capability and a generous payload capacity.
