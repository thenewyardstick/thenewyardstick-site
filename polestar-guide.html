Polestar Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Polestar has evolved from Volvo's performance division into a standalone premium electric car brand focused on minimalist Scandinavian design, cutting-edge technology and long-range EVs. Sharing engineering with Volvo and Geely while developing its own identity, Polestar has rapidly expanded from a single fastback into a growing range of SUVs and grand tourers. Clean Scandinavian styling, high-quality interiors, impressive performance and a strong focus on innovation have established Polestar as one of Europe's leading premium electric vehicle manufacturers. 

Current Models

Polestar 2 (20-)

RRP From : £44,950
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £11,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4606 × 1859 × 1479 mm
Boot : 405L

Key rivals : BMW i4, Hyundai Ioniq 6, Mercedes-Benz CLA, Peugeot E-408, Tesla Model 3

The Polestar 2 combines understated Scandinavian styling with a premium cabin, engaging driving dynamics and impressive electric efficiency. Continual software updates and regular battery improvements have helped it remain competitive since launch, while a choice of single and dual-motor versions ensures there's an option for both efficiency-minded drivers and performance enthusiasts.

Polestar 3 (24-)

RRP From : £76,540
Pre Reg From : c. £45,500
Used Price From : c. £37,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4900 × 1968 × 1614 mm
Boot : 484L

Key rivals : Audi Q8 e-tron, BMW iX, Maserati Grecale Folgore, MG IM6, Volvo EX60

The Polestar 3 brings the brand's clean Scandinavian design language to the premium SUV segment. Strong performance, a luxurious interior and advanced driver assistance technology are complemented by long-range battery options, making it an appealing alternative to established premium electric SUVs.

Polestar 4 (24-)

RRP From : £56,750
Pre Reg From : c. £37,000
Used Price From : c. £30,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4840 × 2008 × 1534 mm
Boot : 526L

Key rivals : BMW i4, DS No8, Lexus RZ, Mazda 6e, Tesla Model Y

The Polestar 4 blurs the line between SUV and fastback, prioritising sleek styling and aerodynamic efficiency. Its camera-based rear visibility system, spacious cabin and generous boot help it stand out, while powerful dual-motor versions deliver genuine sports car performance alongside impressive long-distance capability.

Polestar 5 (26-)

RRP From : £89,500
Pre Reg From : c. £89,500
Used Price From : c. £89,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4998 × 2024 × 1479 mm
Boot : 476L (est.)

Key rivals : BMW i5, Lexus ES, Lotus Emeya, Mercedes-Benz EQE, Volvo ES90

The Polestar 5 is the brand's flagship grand tourer, combining sleek four-door fastback styling with an all-new bonded aluminium platform developed specifically for high-performance electric vehicles. Long-range battery technology, ultra-fast charging and a lightweight bonded aluminium platform give Polestar the ingredients to challenge the very best premium electric GTs on the market.
