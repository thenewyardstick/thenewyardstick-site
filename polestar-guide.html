Polestar Guide

Polestar has evolved from Volvo's performance division into a standalone premium electric car brand focused on minimalist Scandinavian design, cutting-edge technology and long-range EVs. Sharing engineering with Volvo and Geely while developing its own identity, Polestar has rapidly expanded from a single fastback into a growing range of SUVs and grand tourers.

Clean Scandinavian styling, high-quality interiors, impressive performance and a strong focus on innovation have established Polestar as one of Europe's leading premium electric vehicle manufacturers.

Polestar 4 coupé (24-)

Used Price From : c. £30,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4840 × 2008 × 1534 mm
Boot : 526 litres

Key rivals : BMW i4, DS No8, Lexus RZ, Mazda 6e, Tesla Model Y

The Polestar 4 coupé blurs the line between SUV and fastback, prioritising sleek styling and aerodynamic efficiency. Its unusual camera-based rear visibility system replaces a conventional rear window, creating a distinctive passenger environment while retaining a spacious cabin and generous boot. Powerful dual-motor versions deliver genuine sports car performance alongside impressive long-distance capability.

Key rivals : Audi Q6 E-Tron, BMW iX3, Lexus RZ, Maserati Grecale Folgore, Volvo EX60

The Polestar 4 SUV expands the 4 range with a more practical alternative to the existing coupé. It retains the same distinctive Scandinavian design and strong electric performance, but introduces a physical rear window, additional luggage space and a slightly taller body. Rear- and dual-motor versions are available, with the most efficient model offering up to 391 miles of WLTP range.

Polestar 5 (26-)

RRP From : £89,500
Pre Reg From : c. £89,500
Used Price From : c. £89,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4998 × 2024 × 1479 mm
Boot : 476 litres (est.)

Key rivals : BMW i5, Lexus ES, Lotus Emeya, Mercedes-Benz EQE, Volvo ES90

The Polestar 5 is the brand's flagship grand tourer, combining sleek four-door fastback styling with an all-new bonded aluminium platform developed specifically for high-performance electric vehicles. Its 800-volt electrical architecture enables charging at up to 350kW, while the range extends to as much as 421 miles. With up to 871bhp available, it gives Polestar a genuine flagship capable of competing with the fastest premium electric GTs.
