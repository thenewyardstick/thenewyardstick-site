Peugeot Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Peugeot covers a broad range of passenger transport needs, from compact nine-seat Expert Combi models to larger Boxer-based minibuses. Diesel and electric versions are available at both ends of the range, while specialist UK converters offer Boxer and E-Boxer layouts for schools, community transport, care organisations, private hire and wheelchair-accessible work.

Peugeot Expert Combi

The Expert Combi is the passenger-carrying version of Peugeot's mid-sized van, combining seating for up to nine people with the compact dimensions and driving position of a conventional commercial vehicle. Flexible seating and two body lengths make it suitable for hotel transfers, private hire, sports teams and larger families, while twin sliding side doors make access easier in tight urban spaces.

Peugeot E-Expert Combi

The E-Expert Combi brings electric propulsion to Peugeot's compact passenger-van format, retaining the same useful footprint and flexible interior while eliminating local tailpipe emissions. Its quiet drivetrain and easy low-speed operation make it particularly well suited to airport, hotel and urban shuttle work, while the relatively low overall height allows it to access many car parks that are unsuitable for larger minibuses.

Peugeot Boxer Minibus

The Boxer provides the foundation for Peugeot's larger minibus conversions, offering considerably more passenger and wheelchair flexibility than the smaller Expert. Specialist UK converters provide layouts for schools, community transport, care organisations and private hire, with removable seating, passenger lifts and tracked floors available for accessible applications. Different body lengths allow seating and luggage space to be tailored to the operator's requirements.

Peugeot E-Boxer Minibus

The E-Boxer Minibus combines Peugeot's largest electric van with specialist passenger conversions for community, shuttle and accessible transport. The current generation uses a substantially larger battery and stronger electric motor than the earlier E-Boxer, giving operators a more practical electric alternative for longer daily routes. UK conversions can accommodate up to 16 passengers, with wheelchair-accessible layouts available using removable seating and an electric passenger lift.
