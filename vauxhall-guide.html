Vauxhall Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Vauxhall has undergone a significant shift in quality and desirability since joining PSA, and subsequently Stellantis. Models such as the Astra, Mokka and Grandland now combine distinctive styling with increasingly competitive interiors, technology and electrified powertrains. Yet Vauxhall has wisely avoided abandoning the value-conscious British families that built its reputation.

The new Frontera is perhaps the clearest example: a practical family SUV offering hybrid or electric power, seven-seat availability and sensible pricing rather than chasing the premium market. Alongside the ever-popular Corsa and hugely practical Combo and Vivaro Life, today's Vauxhall range feels broader and more relevant than it has for years.

Current Models

Vauxhall Corsa (19-)

RRP From : £20,590
Pre Reg From : c. £15,000
Used Price From : c. £6,000

Powertrains : Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4060 × 1765 × 1433 mm
Boot : 309L / 267L EV

Key rivals : Fiat Grande Panda, MG3, Peugeot 208, Renault Clio, SEAT Ibiza

One of Britain's best-known superminis remains a compelling all-rounder, helped considerably by the discounts commonly available against its official price. Petrol, hybrid and electric versions provide plenty of choice, while the latest Corsa GSE adds a genuinely serious 281PS electric hot hatch to a range that otherwise concentrates on affordable everyday motoring.

Vauxhall Mokka (21-)

RRP From : £26,545
Pre Reg From : c. £18,000
Used Price From : c. £10,000

Powertrains : Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4151 × 1791 × 1534 mm
Boot : 350L, 310L EV

Key rivals : Ford Puma, Jeep Avenger, Nissan Juke, Peugeot 2008, Renault Captur

The second-generation Mokka marked a major change in direction for Vauxhall, introducing the Vizor design language and a far more distinctive appearance than its predecessor. Compact dimensions make it easy to live with, while petrol, hybrid and electric choices broaden its appeal. The 281PS Mokka GSE now gives this small SUV an unexpectedly serious performance flagship.

Vauxhall Frontera (24-)

RRP From : £23,295
Pre Reg From : c. £17,000
Used Price From : c. £15,000

Powertrains : MHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4385 × 1795 × 1635 mm
Boot : 460L

Key rivals : Citroën C3 Aircross, Dacia Duster, MG ZS, Renault Symbioz, Volkswagen Taigo

Frontera takes Vauxhall back towards its traditional strengths: straightforward, spacious family transport at a sensible price. Hybrid versions can even accommodate seven seats, while the electric model undercuts numerous smaller EVs. It isn't trying to masquerade as a premium SUV, and that arguably makes the practical, value-focused Frontera one of the most relevant cars in Vauxhall's current range.

Vauxhall Astra (22-)

RRP From : £26,495
Pre Reg From : c. £18,500
Used Price From : c. £12,000

Powertrains : Petrol, MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4374, 4642 Sports Tourer × 1860 × 1465 mm
Boot : 422L, 352L EV, 310L PHEV, 597L Sports Tourer

Key rivals :  Kia K4, Leapmotor B05, Peugeot 308, SEAT Leon, Toyota Corolla

Sharp styling and a substantially more modern interior transformed the Astra into one of Vauxhall's most desirable mainstream cars. Its broad powertrain range is another strength, spanning conventional petrol through hybrid, plug-in hybrid and fully electric versions. It remains sensibly sized too, providing a useful alternative for buyers who don't necessarily want another SUV.

Vauxhall Grandland (24-)

RRP From : £34,700
Pre Reg From : c. £25,000
Used Price From : c. £20,500

Powertrains : MHEV, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4650 × 1905 × 1665 mm
Boot : 550L

Key rivals : Dacia Bigster, Hyundai Tucson, Kia Sportage, Renault Austral, Volkswagen Tiguan

The latest Grandland is considerably more ambitious than its predecessor, with a striking illuminated front end, a much improved cabin and far greater road presence. Prices - especially pre‑reg deals - are among the best in class, strengthening its appeal. A 325PS dual‑motor AWD version provides an unexpectedly potent range‑topper.

Vauxhall Combo Life (18-)

RRP From : £30,840
Pre Reg From : c. £20,000
Used Price From : c. £12,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4403, 4753 XL × 1848 × 1841 mm
Boot : 775L, 1050L XL

Key rivals : Citroën ë-Berlingo, Ford E-Tourneo Courier, Peugeot E-Rifter, Toyota Proace City Verso

The Combo Life doesn’t try to hide its van origins, and that’s exactly why it works so well. It’s massive inside, with practicality that puts far larger and more expensive SUVs to shame. Pre‑reg deals make it one of the most affordable options for families needing maximum cargo capacity without sacrificing everyday usability.

Vauxhall Vivaro Life (19-)

RRP From : £35,400
Pre Reg From : c. £35,400
Used Price From : c. £11,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4981, 5306 XL × 1920 × 1905 mm
Boot : 989L, 1300L XL

Key rivals : Citroën ë-SpaceTourer, Ford E-Tourneo Custom, Kia PV5, Peugeot E-Traveller, Toyota Proace Verso

The Vivaro Life Electric suits buyers who need far more than five seats, offering vast passenger space for up to nine people in both Standard and XL body lengths. Its WLTP range of up to 214 miles and very competitive pricing strengthen its appeal, though savvy buyers may be tempted by the Toyota Proace, which offers a similar package with up to 10 years of warranty cover.
