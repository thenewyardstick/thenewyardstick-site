Volvo 9700 & 9700 Double Decker

The 9700 family represents Volvo's premium touring-coach offering, combining powerful 13-litre diesel engines with sophisticated safety technology and comfortable passenger accommodation. The single-deck 9700 returned to European production in October 2026 with revised suspension and three body lengths, although availability of the relaunched model in Britain remains to be confirmed. The larger 9700 Double Decker is already established in the UK, with 43 examples ordered for Scottish Citylink and Megabus services. Built with specialist bodywork from Finland's Carrus Delta, the double-decker offers up to 97 seats, making it particularly suitable for long-distance express services and routes requiring exceptional passenger capacity.

Volvo B8R MCV eVoTor

The B8R MCV eVoTor is Volvo's practical, cost-conscious full-sized coach, developed specifically for British and Irish operators with bodywork from Egyptian manufacturer MCV. Its proven eight-litre diesel engine and comfortable interior make it suitable for private hire, rail replacement, tourism and school transport. Conventional touring versions provide around 53–55 passenger seats, with additional layouts available. A new high-capacity variant introduced for 2026 expands the range to 59 or 72 seats, using either conventional 2+2 or higher-density 3+2 seating. This makes the eVoTor particularly versatile for operators needing a reliable vehicle that can serve different types of passenger contracts.

Volvo B13R UNVI XL

Developed specifically for Britain and Ireland, the B13R UNVI XL combines Volvo's latest heavy-duty touring chassis with premium bodywork from Spanish manufacturer UNVI. The 12.8-metre coach offers 53 passenger seats, or 49 alongside wheelchair accommodation, with a comfortable touring interior, individual USB charging and a centre toilet. Volvo's 500hp 13-litre engine and I-Shift transmission provide strong performance for long-distance journeys, while modern active safety systems and Volvo's established support network appeal to operators seeking a premium vehicle. Series production began in Portugal during 2026. The UNVI coachbuilder guide contains a more detailed description of the body and passenger equipment.

Volvo MCV City Buses – eVoRa & eVoSeti

Volvo's diesel city-bus range includes two established MCV-bodied families designed for intensive public transport and high-capacity passenger work. The B8RLE eVoRa is a low-entry single-decker available in several lengths, with accessible interiors and up to 43 fixed passenger seats in standard layouts. The larger B8L eVoSeti is a three-axle double-decker offering up to 98 seats, making it particularly suitable for school contracts, rail replacement and heavily used regional services. Both use Volvo's reliable D8K diesel engine, automatic transmissions and modern safety equipment. New examples of both families continued entering British service during 2025 and 2026.

Volvo BZL Electric

The BZL Electric is Volvo's principal battery-electric city-bus family for Britain, offered with MCV bodywork in both single- and double-deck configurations. Its flexible single-deck platform covers seven lengths from nine to thirteen metres, while the double-decker provides up to 65 seats within a 10.9-metre body. Modular batteries, rapid charging and regenerative braking allow operators to configure vehicles around individual routes and depot facilities. The range is already firmly established in Britain, with more than 180 BZL Electric buses operating within Greater Manchester's Bee Network by early 2026. Both versions offer low-floor accessibility, modern passenger equipment and Volvo's comprehensive driver-assistance technology.

Important Information

Prices, specifications, dimensions, seating capacities, weights, power outputs and equipment are based primarily on information published by vehicle manufacturers, their official UK representatives and established specialist coachbuilders. Specifications can vary by chassis, body length, drivetrain, configuration, model year and individual vehicle. Where a passenger vehicle is produced or modified by a third-party converter, specifications and pricing may vary substantially between suppliers.

Prices shown are UK prices where available and are intended as a guide. Where an official UK list price is unavailable, pricing may be shown as POA. Prices can change and may exclude optional equipment, conversions, delivery charges or other operator-specific costs.

The Volvo 9700 single-deck coach was relaunched for Europe in October 2026, but confirmed British right-hand-drive new-order availability for the relaunched generation has not been established. Other models featured have confirmed UK sales, deliveries or manufacturer support, although individual specifications and delivery slots may vary.

Passenger seating capacities must not be confused with total passenger capacities. Urban buses may accommodate standing passengers, while wheelchair accommodation, toilets, luggage facilities and executive seating can reduce the number of available passenger seats. High-capacity 3+2 school seating is not directly comparable with conventional touring arrangements.

Licence categories shown are a general guide only. Individual driving entitlement can vary according to passenger-seat count, vehicle weight, vehicle length, licence issue date, grandfather rights, Section 19 operation and other exemptions. Passenger-seat counts used for licence purposes exclude the driver. Always check the vehicle's plated specification and your own driving-licence entitlement before driving.

Electric battery capacities, charging rates and driving ranges are manufacturer figures and depend on specification and operating conditions. Real-world electric range will vary with passenger load, speed, temperature, heating, route characteristics and battery condition.

Specifications are checked against the latest information reasonably available to The New Yardstick at the time of publication or update. Manufacturers, chassis suppliers and coachbuilders may alter specifications, pricing or availability without notice.
