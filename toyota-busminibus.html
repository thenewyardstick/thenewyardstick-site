Toyota Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Toyota's current UK passenger-van offering centres on the Proace Verso, an electric people carrier available with up to nine seats. Closely related to the Stellantis medium-van family, it combines a car-like cabin with sliding side doors, flexible rear seating and enough space for family, shuttle and group-transport use. Toyota's Service Activated Warranty can also provide cover for up to 10 years or 100,000 miles, subject to eligible Toyota servicing.


Toyota Proace Verso

The Proace Verso is Toyota's largest factory-built passenger vehicle in the UK, offering seating for up to nine occupants in Long Icon specification. Its electric drivetrain makes it particularly well suited to hotel transfers, airport work, larger families and organisations needing a spacious people carrier without moving to a full-size minibus. Medium and Long bodies are available, with different seating layouts depending on trim.
