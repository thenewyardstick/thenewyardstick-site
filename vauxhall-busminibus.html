Vauxhall Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Vauxhall covers passenger transport from the factory-built Vivaro Life Electric through to larger Movano-based minibuses produced by specialist UK converters. The Vivaro Life offers up to nine seats in a car-like electric people carrier, while diesel and electric Movano conversions provide additional capacity for schools, community groups, private hire and accessible transport.


Vauxhall Vivaro Life Electric Combi

The Vivaro Life Electric is Vauxhall's factory-built electric passenger van, combining up to nine seats with twin sliding side doors and the relatively manageable dimensions of a medium-sized van. Combi models prioritise passenger capacity, while higher trims offer more car-like seating and equipment. It suits larger families, hotel and airport transfers, private hire and organisations needing a spacious zero-emission people carrier.

Price From: c.£36,900

Powertrain: Electric

Power: 100 kW / 136 hp

Battery: 50 kWh / 75 kWh, specification dependent

Range: Up to 230 miles WLTP, specification dependent

Charging: Up to 100 kW DC rapid charging

Transmission: Single-speed automatic

Seats: Up to 9 occupants

Typical Licence: B

Drive: Front-wheel drive

Body Lengths: Medium / XL

Typical Use: Larger families, hotel transfers, airport shuttles, private hire and group transport

Vauxhall Movano Minibus

The Movano provides the basis for larger Vauxhall minibus conversions, with specialist UK converters offering passenger layouts for schools, community transport, private hire and accessible services. Its large square body allows flexible seating arrangements, tracked floors, wheelchair spaces and passenger-lift installations, while diesel power remains useful for operators covering longer daily distances.

Powertrain: Diesel

Vauxhall Movano Electric Minibus

The Movano Electric can also be converted into a zero-emission minibus for school, community, care and shuttle work. Current UK conversions include layouts for up to 14 occupants, with wheelchair-accessible configurations also available. It uses the latest Movano Electric platform, giving operators a large passenger vehicle without a diesel engine and making particular sense for predictable daily routes and urban or clean-air-zone operation.

Price From: POA / conversion dependent

Base Vehicle: Vauxhall Movano Electric

Powertrain: Electric

Power: Up to 200 kW / 270 hp

Battery: Up to 110 kWh, specification dependent

Range: Up to 263 miles WLTP for the base vehicle, conversion dependent

Charging: AC and DC charging

Seats: Up to 14 occupants on current UK conversions

Typical Licence: D1 / D, depending on seating and vehicle specification

Drive: Front-wheel drive

Accessibility: Wheelchair-accessible layouts available with rear ramps or electric passenger lifts

Typical Use: Schools, community transport, care services, hotel and airport shuttles and zero-emission accessible transport
