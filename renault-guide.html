Renault Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Renault has successfully reinvented itself by combining distinctive French design with efficient hybrid and electric technology. Alongside popular family cars and SUVs, the brand is reviving iconic names such as the 4 and 5 as modern EVs, while continuing to develop one of Europe's strongest hybrid line-ups. Competitive pricing, comfortable ride quality and clever packaging ensure Renault remains one of the UK's most compelling mainstream automotive brands.

Current Models

Renault Clio (19-)

RRP From : £17,995
Pre Reg From : c. £14,000
Used Price From : c. £8,000

Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid
Dimensions : 4053 × 1798 × 1439 mm
Boot : 391L, 301L Hybrid

Key rivals : Fiat Grande Panda, MG3, Peugeot 208, SEAT Ibiza, Toyota Yaris

The Clio remains one of Europe's benchmark superminis, combining smart styling, impressive refinement and one of the strongest full hybrid systems in the class. Recent price reductions have strengthened its value credentials further, helping Renault offer one of the UK's best-value premium small cars.

Renault Captur (20-)

RRP From : £23,995
Pre Reg From : c. £17,500
Used Price From : c. £11,000

Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid
Dimensions : 4239 × 1797 × 1576 mm
Boot : 484L, 440L Hybrid

Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Junior, Ford Puma, Peugeot 2008, SEAT Arona, Toyota Yaris Cross

The Captur combines compact SUV styling with impressive practicality, helped by a sliding rear bench and one of the largest boots in the class. Efficient hybrid powertrains, competitive pricing and a comfortable ride continue to make it a popular family choice.

Renault 5 (25-)

RRP From : £22,995
Pre Reg From : c. £20,000
Used Price From : c. £19,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 3922 × 1774 × 1498 mm
Boot : 326L

Key rivals : Alpine A290, Citroën ë-C3, Fiat Grande Panda, MINI Cooper Electric, Peugeot E-208

The Renault 5 successfully blends retro-inspired styling with modern electric technology, offering impressive efficiency, sharp handling and standout design. Inspired by one of Renault's most iconic small cars, it combines characterful styling with a high-quality cabin, advanced Google-based infotainment and everyday usability. Long-range battery options and an engaging driving experience ensure the reborn 5 offers far more than nostalgic looks alone.

Renault 4 (25-)

RRP From : £27,995
Pre Reg From : c. £23,000
Used Price From : c. £21,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4143 × 1800 × 1570 mm
Boot : 420L

Key rivals : Ford Puma Gen-e, Jeep Avenger, Kia EV3, MINI Aceman, Volvo EX30

The reborn Renault 4 transforms one of the brand's most recognisable names into a practical compact electric crossover. A generous boot, spacious cabin and distinctive styling give buyers a practical alternative to the smaller Renault 5.

Renault Mégane E-Tech (22-)

RRP From : £32,995
Pre Reg From : c. £26,000
Used Price From : c. £12,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4199 × 1768 × 1505 mm
Boot : 440L

Key rivals : BYD Dolphin, Kia EV3, Leapmotor B05, MG4, Volkswagen ID.3

The Mégane E-Tech remains one of the most refined family EVs available, combining a spacious cabin, Google-based infotainment and a high-quality interior. Excellent efficiency, strong refinement and increasingly attractive pre-registered prices continue to make it one of the best-value premium family EVs available.

Renault Symbioz (24-)

RRP From : £29,995
Pre Reg From : c. £23,500
Used Price From : c. £19,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4413 × 1797 × 1573 mm
Boot : 624L

Key rivals : Hyundai Kona, Kia Niro, MG ZS, Nissan Qashqai, Vauxhall Frontera

The Symbioz fills the gap between the Captur and Austral, offering one of the largest boots in the class without becoming significantly larger to drive. Efficient hybrid power and family-friendly practicality make it an impressive all-rounder.

Renault Scénic E-Tech (24-)

RRP From : £37,995
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £24,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4470 × 1864 × 1571 mm
Boot : 545L

Key rivals : Citroën ë-C5 Aircross, Kia EV5, MG S5, Leapmotor B10, Volkswagen ID.4

The Scénic E-Tech reinvents an iconic Renault name as a spacious family EV. Strong efficiency, long-range battery options and a practical interior make it one of the most convincing electric family SUVs currently available.

Renault Austral (23-)

RRP From : £35,995
Pre Reg From : c. £28,500
Used Price From : c. £22,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4510 × 1825 × 1621 mm
Boot : 555L

Key rivals : Dacia Bigster, Hyundai Tucson, Jaecoo 7, Vauxhall Grandland, Volkswagen Tiguan

The Austral offers one of the strongest hybrid powertrains in the medium SUV class alongside a refined cabin and impressive comfort. Competitive pricing and generous equipment help it stand out in one of Europe's most fiercely contested market segments.

Renault Rafale (24-)

RRP From : £42,995
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £23,500

Powertrains : Hybrid, PHEV
Dimensions : 4710 × 1886 × 1613 mm
Boot : 627L, 530L PHEV

Key rivals : DS No7, Lexus NX, Mazda CX-60, Peugeot 408, Volvo XC60

The Rafale sits at the top of Renault's SUV range, combining sleek fastback styling with a spacious interior and one of the largest boots in its class. Efficient hybrid and powerful plug-in hybrid powertrains deliver strong performance without sacrificing everyday usability, while substantial pre-registered discounts make Renault's flagship feel like particularly impressive value.
