Renault Guide

Renault has successfully reinvented itself by combining distinctive French design with efficient hybrid and electric technology. Alongside popular family cars and SUVs, the brand is reviving iconic names such as the 4 and 5 as modern EVs, while continuing to develop one of Europe's strongest hybrid line-ups.

Competitive pricing, comfortable ride quality and clever packaging ensure Renault remains one of the UK's most compelling mainstream automotive brands.

Current Models

Renault Clio (19-)

RRP From : £20,995
Pre Reg From : c. £14,000
Used Price From : c. £8,000

Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid
Dimensions : 4053 × 1798 × 1439 mm
Boot : 391 litres, 301 litres Hybrid

Key rivals : Fiat Grande Panda, MG3, Peugeot 208, SEAT Ibiza, Toyota Yaris

The Clio remains one of Europe's benchmark superminis, combining smart styling, impressive refinement and one of the strongest full hybrid systems in the class. The current generation remains available while Renault prepares its all-new successor.

Renault Captur (20-)

RRP From : £22,495
Pre Reg From : c. £17,500
Used Price From : c. £11,000

Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid
Dimensions : 4239 × 1797 × 1576 mm
Boot : 484 litres, 440 litres Hybrid

Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Junior, Ford Puma, Peugeot 2008, SEAT Arona, Toyota Yaris Cross

The Captur combines compact SUV styling with impressive practicality, helped by a sliding rear bench and one of the largest boots in the class. Petrol and full hybrid powertrains, competitive pricing and a comfortable ride continue to make it a popular family choice.

Renault 5 (25-)

RRP From : £21,495
Pre Reg From : c. £20,000
Used Price From : c. £19,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 3922 × 1774 × 1498 mm
Boot : 326 litres

Key rivals : Alpine A290, Citroën ë-C3, Fiat Grande Panda, MINI Cooper Electric, Peugeot E-208

The Renault 5 successfully blends retro-inspired styling with modern electric technology, offering impressive efficiency, sharp handling and standout design. Inspired by one of Renault's most iconic small cars, it combines characterful styling with a high-quality cabin, advanced infotainment and everyday usability. Buyers can choose between urban- and comfort-range batteries, keeping the 5 competitive at both ends of the electric supermini market.

Renault 4 (25-)

RRP From : £23,445
Pre Reg From : c. £23,000
Used Price From : c. £21,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4143 × 1800 × 1570 mm
Boot : 420 litres

Key rivals : Ford Puma Gen-e, Jeep Avenger, Kia EV3, MINI Aceman, Volvo EX30

The reborn Renault 4 transforms one of the brand's most recognisable names into a practical compact electric crossover. A generous boot, spacious cabin and distinctive styling give buyers a more versatile alternative to the smaller Renault 5, while the optional full-length canvas roof adds another unusual touch.

Renault Mégane E-Tech (22-)

RRP From : £28,245
Pre Reg From : c. £26,000
Used Price From : c. £12,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4199 × 1768 × 1505 mm
Boot : 440 litres

Key rivals : BYD Dolphin, Kia EV3, Leapmotor B05, MG4, Volkswagen ID.3

The refreshed Mégane E-Tech remains one of Renault's most distinctive family EVs, combining a spacious cabin, Google-based infotainment and a high-quality interior. Improved range and strong efficiency keep it competitive, while its compact exterior dimensions and 440-litre boot make it particularly easy to live with.

Renault Symbioz (24-)

RRP From : £27,595
Pre Reg From : c. £23,500
Used Price From : c. £19,000

Powertrains : MHEV, Hybrid
Dimensions : 4413 × 1797 × 1573 mm
Boot : 624 litres

Key rivals : Hyundai Kona, Kia Niro, MG ZS, Nissan Qashqai, Vauxhall Frontera

The Symbioz fills the gap between the Captur and Austral, offering one of the largest boots in the class without becoming significantly larger to drive. The range includes both mild-hybrid and full-hybrid powertrains, broadening its appeal while retaining the family-friendly practicality that is central to the model.

Renault Scénic E-Tech (24-)

RRP From : £33,245
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £24,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4470 × 1864 × 1571 mm
Boot : 545 litres

Key rivals : Citroën ë-C5 Aircross, Kia EV5, MG S5, Leapmotor B10, Volkswagen ID.4

The Scénic E-Tech reinvents an iconic Renault name as a spacious family EV. Its long-range battery delivers close to 400 miles of WLTP range, while a practical interior, generous boot and strong efficiency make it one of the most convincing electric family SUVs currently available.

Renault Austral (23-)

RRP From : £35,495
Pre Reg From : c. £28,500
Used Price From : c. £22,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4510 × 1825 × 1621 mm
Boot : 555 litres

Key rivals : Dacia Bigster, Hyundai Tucson, Jaecoo 7, Vauxhall Grandland, Volkswagen Tiguan

The updated Austral offers one of the strongest hybrid powertrains in the medium SUV class alongside a refined cabin and impressive comfort. Its full-hybrid system, updated styling and generous equipment help it stand out in one of Europe's most fiercely contested market segments.

Renault Rafale (24-)

RRP From : £38,495
Pre Reg From : c. £31,000
Used Price From : c. £23,500

Powertrains : Hybrid, PHEV
Dimensions : 4710 × 1886 × 1613 mm
Boot : 627 litres, 530 litres PHEV

Key rivals : DS No7, Lexus NX, Mazda CX-60, Peugeot 408, Volvo XC60

The Rafale sits at the top of Renault's SUV range, combining sleek fastback styling with a spacious interior and one of the largest boots in its class. The full hybrid provides an efficient entry point, while the more powerful plug-in hybrid adds four-wheel drive and substantially stronger performance without sacrificing everyday usability.

Renault Trafic Passenger (16-)

RRP From : £45,385
Pre Reg From : c. £29,000
Used Price From : c. £7,500

Powertrains : Diesel
Dimensions : 5080, (5480 LWB) × 1956 × 1973 mm
Boot : 1,050 litres SWB (9 seats), 1,800 litres LWB (9 seats), up to 6,000 litres with all rear seats removed 

Key rivals : Citroën Spacetourer, Ford Tourneo Custom, Kia PV5, Toyota Proace Verso, Volkswagen Multivan

The Trafic Passenger gives Renault a genuine large people carrier, offering seating for up to nine and considerably more luggage space than a conventional seven-seat SUV. The current model remains diesel-powered, with short- and long-wheelbase versions and manual or automatic gearboxes available. Its van-based origins bring huge interior space and sliding side doors, making it particularly well suited to large families, airport transfers and other passenger-carrying duties.
