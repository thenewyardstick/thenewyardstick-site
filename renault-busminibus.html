Renault Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Renault covers passenger transport from the compact nine-seat Trafic Passenger to larger Master-based minibuses used by schools, community groups and private-hire operators. Diesel versions remain the mainstay of the range, while the latest Master E-Tech Electric also provides a modern zero-emission platform for specialist passenger conversions.

Renault Trafic Passenger

The Trafic Passenger is Renault's factory-built nine-seat passenger van, combining three rows of seating with the manageable dimensions and driving position of a conventional mid-sized van. Short- and long-wheelbase versions are available, while removable rear seating allows passenger space to be traded for luggage capacity. It suits larger families, hotel transfers, sports teams, private hire and organisations that need more seats without moving to a full-size minibus.

Renault Master Minibus

The Master provides the basis for Renault's larger minibus conversions, giving operators much more flexibility than the smaller Trafic Passenger. UK converters offer layouts ranging from compact nine-passenger versions to 17-passenger minibuses, with removable seating, tracked floors and wheelchair-accessible configurations available. The latest Master also brings a more modern cab and updated diesel engines while retaining the large, square passenger area that makes it well suited to school, community and private-hire work.

Renault Master E-Tech Electric Minibus

The Master E-Tech Electric provides a modern zero-emission base for specialist minibus conversions, using the same new-generation Master body as the diesel model but replacing its engine with Renault's latest electric drivetrain. Its large battery and substantially improved range make it much more practical than earlier electric Master conversions, particularly for community transport, shuttle services and accessible routes with predictable daily mileage.

Renault Master Shuttle E-Tech

The Master Shuttle E-Tech takes Renault's new-generation electric Master well beyond a conventional van conversion. Designed and manufactured in France by Renault Vehicle Innovation, it uses a dedicated full low-floor passenger architecture with 14 configurable seats and is classified as an M2 Class B vehicle. It is aimed at on-demand transport, suburban and rural routes, hotel and airport shuttles and other passenger services that need something more specialised than a conventional Master minibus without moving to a larger bus.
