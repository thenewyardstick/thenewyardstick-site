Koenigsegg Guide

Founded in Sweden in 1994 by Christian von Koenigsegg, Koenigsegg has grown from a small, ambitious start-up into one of the world's most technically adventurous hypercar manufacturers. Producing just a handful of cars each year, the company has built its reputation around extraordinary performance, lightweight construction and innovative engineering.

From the early CC8S and record-breaking Agera to the Regera, Jesko and four-seat Gemera, Koenigsegg has consistently challenged conventional supercar design. Its in-house engines, advanced carbon-fibre construction and unconventional transmission technology demonstrate a willingness to develop entirely new solutions rather than follow established industry practices.

Every Koenigsegg is exceptionally rare, expensive and engineered to push the boundaries of what a road car can achieve.

Current Models

Dimensions: 4626 × 2030 × 1210 mm
Boot: 100L (50L front + 50L rear)

Key rivals: Aston Martin Valkyrie, Bugatti Tourbillon, Czinger 21C, Gordon Murray T.50, Pagani Utopia

The Jesko Attack is the winged, circuit-minded member of the Jesko family. Its dramatic aerodynamics and fast-shifting gearbox suit an owner who wants a road-legal hypercar with genuine track-day intent. There are two seats and a detachable roof, although the roof cannot be carried aboard. It is an occasion car with the technical theatre to match its appearance.

Jesko Absolut (25-)

RRP From: c. £3,400,000
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: N/A

Powertrains: Petrol
Dimensions: 4845 × 2030 × 1210 mm
Boot: 150L (100L front + 50L rear)

Key rivals: Aston Martin Valkyrie, Bugatti Tourbillon, McLaren W1, Rimac Nevera, SSC Tuatara

The Absolut exchanges the Attack’s huge rear wing for a longer, smoother shape developed around low drag. It is the Jesko for an owner fascinated by high-speed engineering, although its potential belongs far beyond British public-road limits. The cabin remains a two-seat affair, with the same spectacular doors and a little more luggage space than its winged sibling.

CC850 (26-)

RRP From: c. £4,400,000
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: N/A

Powertrains: Petrol
Dimensions: 4364 × 2024 × 1127 mm
Boot: N/A

Key rivals: Bugatti Tourbillon, Czinger 21C, Gordon Murray T.50, McLaren W1, Pagani Utopia

The CC850 revisits the clean shape of Koenigsegg’s early cars but adds a particularly unusual party trick. Its transmission offers a clutch pedal and manual-style shifting alongside an automatic mode. That makes it an intriguing choice for collectors who enjoy operating the machinery as much as admiring it. A removable roof and restrained styling give it a different flavour from a Jesko.

Sadair's Spear (26-)

RRP From: c. £4,400,000
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: N/A

Powertrains: Petrol
Dimensions: 4690 × 2030 × 1210 mm
Boot: 100L (50L front + 50L rear)

Key rivals: Aston Martin Valkyrie, Czinger 21C, Gordon Murray T.50, McLaren W1, Mercedes-AMG One

Sadair’s Spear takes Koenigsegg’s track-focused thinking further, with more aggressive aerodynamics and a tightly limited production run. It remains a road-legal two-seater, but its purpose is much closer to a special circuit day than a relaxed cross-country tour. For a collector already familiar with the brand, the appeal is its more concentrated character and rarity.

Gemera (26-)

RRP From: c. £2,800,000
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: N/A

Powertrains: PHEV
Dimensions: 4975 × 1995 × 1295mm
Boot: 200L

Key rivals: Aston Martin Valhalla, Bugatti Tourbillon, Ferrari F80, McLaren W1, Rimac Nevera

The Gemera is the unusual Koenigsegg that makes room for four people. Its large doors open into a cabin intended to carry adults and their hand luggage, giving the brand’s extraordinary engineering a more sociable purpose. It is still an exotic collector’s car, but the attraction is sharing the journey rather than asking everyone else to follow in something ordinary.
