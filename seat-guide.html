SEAT Guide

SEAT's UK range is now tightly focused around three core models: the Ibiza, Arona and Leon. Rather than abandoning its established line-up, the Spanish manufacturer has continued to develop it, with substantial updates bringing fresher styling, improved interiors and newer technology.

The Ibiza and Arona were both refreshed for 2026, while the Leon continues to offer one of the broadest powertrain choices in the family hatchback class. Attractive pricing, distinctive styling and straightforward everyday usability remain central to SEAT's appeal, giving buyers a value-focused alternative within the Volkswagen Group.

Current Models

SEAT Ibiza (17-)

RRP From : £21,610
Pre Reg From : c. £16,500
Used Price From : c. £4,500

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4059 × 1780 × 1447 mm
Boot : 355 litres

Key rivals : Dacia Sandero, Fiat Grande Panda, Hyundai i20, Suzuki Swift, Vauxhall Corsa

The Ibiza may now be one of the older superminis on sale, but another substantial update for 2026 has kept it looking fresh. Revised exterior styling and an improved interior join the qualities that were already there: compact dimensions, enjoyable handling and useful practicality. A straightforward petrol range also keeps things relatively simple in a market increasingly dominated by electrification.

SEAT Arona (17-)

RRP From : £23,805
Pre Reg From : c. £16,000
Used Price From : c. £4,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4154 × 1780 × 1537 mm
Boot : 400 litres

Key rivals : Ford Puma, Jeep Avenger, Nissan Juke, Renault Captur, Suzuki Vitara

The Arona combines compact SUV proportions with useful interior space, making it an easy everyday choice for buyers who want something taller than an Ibiza without moving into a much larger family SUV. The 2026 refresh brings sharper styling and an improved cabin, while a range of turbocharged petrol engines includes a punchy 150PS 1.5-litre option with DSG automatic transmission.

SEAT Leon (20-)

RRP From : £25,995
Pre Reg From : c. £19,000
Used Price From : c. £7,500

Powertrains : Petrol, Diesel, MHEV, PHEV
Dimensions : 4368 × 1799 × 1456 mm
Boot : 380 litres, 620 litres Estate, 270 litres PHEV

Key rivals : Citroën C4, Kia K4, Mazda 3, Toyota Corolla, Vauxhall Astra

The Leon remains SEAT's most versatile model, available as both a hatchback and spacious estate with petrol, diesel, mild-hybrid and plug-in hybrid powertrains. The latest e-HYBRID can travel more than 80 miles on electric power under WLTP testing, while conventional engines keep the entry price competitive. A comfortable chassis, practical cabin and broad range make the Leon a particularly well-rounded family car.
