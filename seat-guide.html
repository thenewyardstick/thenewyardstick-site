SEAT Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

SEAT is entering a new chapter. Following a period of uncertainty and a gradually ageing model range, the Spanish manufacturer now appears to be preparing a fresh generation of products while continuing to offer great value across its line-up. Combining attractive pricing with distinctive styling, comfortable road manners and modern cabin technology, the Ibiza, Leon and Arona remain compelling choices for buyers looking to maximise value without sacrificing quality. If SEAT's rumoured renewal plans come to fruition, the brand could soon combine that value with an exciting new generation of models.

Current Models

SEAT Ibiza (17-)

RRP From : £20,015
Pre Reg From : c. £16,500
Used Price From : c. £4,500

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4059 × 1780 × 1447 mm
Boot : 355L

Key rivals : Dacia Sandero, Fiat Grande Panda, Hyundai i20, Suzuki Swift, Vauxhall Corsa

The SEAT Ibiza may be nearly a decade old, but steady updates keep it competitive. Its compact dimensions make it easy to live with, while decent handling and surprisingly good practicality help it punch above its age. For a small hatchback, it remains a straightforward, competent and genuinely usable everyday choice.

SEAT Arona (17-)

RRP From : £24,015
Pre Reg From : c. £16,000
Used Price From : c. £4,000

Powertrains : Petrol
Dimensions : 4154 × 1780 × 1537 mm
Boot : 400L

Key rivals : Ford Puma, Jeep Avenger, Nissan Juke, Renault Captur, Suzuki Vitara

 

The SEAT Arona is a compact SUV with a hint of MPV‑style packaging, making it a genuinely practical everyday option. Used examples often undercut many rivals, adding to its appeal, and mild‑hybrid technology is due soon, which should noticeably boost refinement and desirability before the next‑generation model arrives.

SEAT Leon (20-)

RRP From : £28,835
Pre Reg From : c. £19,000
Used Price From : c. £7,500

Powertrains : Petrol, Diesel, Mild Hybrid, Plug-in Hybrid
Dimensions : 4368 × 1799 × 1456 mm
Boot : 380L, 620L Estate, 270L PHEV

Key rivals : Citroën C4, Kia K4, Mazda 3, Toyota Corolla, Vauxhall Astra

The SEAT Leon offers a wide range of drivetrains, giving buyers plenty of choice while still undercutting many rivals on price. A spacious cabin, good driving dynamics and its Google‑powered touchscreen add to its everyday appeal, making it a straightforward, well‑rounded option in the family‑hatchback class.
