The Best Value Pre-Registered Vans on Sale Right Now

📈 Market Watch 9 Aug 2026

Pre-registered vans continue to offer some of the biggest savings anywhere in the UK new vehicle market, with discounts often reaching tens of thousands of pounds compared with official list prices.

Whether you're a business owner, sole trader or private buyer, these nearly-new vehicles can deliver exceptional value without the compromises of buying an older used van.

From compact electric city vans to heavy-duty long-wheelbase workhorses, we've highlighted some of the best pre-registered commercial vehicle deals currently available, comparing official RRPs with typical market prices to show where the biggest savings can be found.


Citroën ë-C3 Van
RRP From : £17,535 + VAT (£21,042 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £13,250 + VAT (£15,900 inc. VAT)

The Citroën ë-C3 Van brings SUV-inspired styling and electric efficiency to the compact van market at an exceptionally competitive price. Despite its compact dimensions, it offers a practical load area and a comfortable driving experience, making it well suited to urban businesses and local deliveries.

Dacia Spring Cargo
RRP From : £13,995 + VAT (£16,794 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £7,995 + VAT (£9,594 inc. VAT)

The Dacia Spring Cargo strips electric van ownership back to the essentials, offering compact dimensions, low running costs and remarkable value. While its modest performance and payload mean it won't suit every business, it's ideal for urban deliveries and local trades.

At typical pre-registered prices, it costs around the same as many five-year-old compact diesel vans, making it one of the cheapest ways to own a brand-new electric commercial vehicle. For businesses that don't need a large van, the Spring Cargo proves that switching to electric can be surprisingly affordable.


Farizon SV
RRP From : £40,000 + VAT (£48,000 inc. VAT)
Pre-Reg Price : c. £24,990 + VAT (£29,988 inc. VAT)

The Farizon SV is one of the most convincing new entrants to the UK electric van market, combining a dedicated EV platform with impressive practicality, refinement and payload capacity. Unlike many rivals adapted from diesel-powered vans, it has been designed as an electric vehicle from the outset, helping maximise efficiency and interior space.

The largest 83kWh battery delivers up to 234 miles of WLTP range, around 17 miles further than the comparable 75kWh Vauxhall Vivaro Electric. Strong refinement, modern technology and competitive operating costs make the Farizon SV a compelling alternative to more established medium electric vans.


Fiat Ducato
RRP From : £35,260 + VAT (£42,312 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £22,490 + VAT (£26,988 inc. VAT)

The Fiat Ducato has long been one of Europe's most successful large vans, offering an enormous choice of body lengths, roof heights and bodystyles, including panel vans, chassis cabs, tippers, curtain-siders and Lutons. Designed for demanding commercial use, it combines impressive payloads with proven diesel engines, while the latest E-Ducato offers up to 263 miles of WLTP range.

Its class-leading width between the rear wheel arches makes it a favourite for motorhome conversions, allowing transverse fixed beds, while its versatility ensures it remains one of the UK's hardest-working large vans.

Ford Transit Courier
RRP From : £17,800 + VAT (£21,360 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £15,990 + VAT (£19,188 inc. VAT)

The Ford Transit Courier combines compact dimensions with one of the boldest designs in Ford's commercial vehicle range, giving it rugged SUV-inspired styling and excellent proportions. Despite its small footprint, it offers an impressive load area that's ideal for urban deliveries and local trades.

Buyers can choose between Ford's economical 1.0-litre EcoBoost petrol engine or a fully electric version, with surprisingly little price difference between the two on the pre-registered market. Comfortable, easy to drive and highly practical, the Transit Courier proves that a compact van doesn't have to compromise on capability or everyday usability.


Iveco Daily
RRP From : £35,260 + VAT (£42,312 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £28,995 + VAT (£34,794 inc. VAT)

The Iveco Daily is built for the toughest jobs, using a traditional body-on-frame chassis that allows it to carry heavier payloads and tackle more demanding work than many monocoque rivals. Available in an enormous range of body lengths, wheelbases and bodystyles, it includes some of the largest panel vans available in the UK.

While often costing more than many competitors, its truck-inspired construction, durability and exceptional versatility make it a favourite with operators carrying heavy loads every day.


Kia PV5 Cargo
RRP From : £27,645 + VAT (£33,174 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £21,495 + VAT (£25,794 inc. VAT)

The Kia PV5 Cargo brings fresh thinking to the medium van market, sacrificing a little outright load space in favour of distinctive styling, modern technology and a dedicated electric platform. Kia has got the design right, creating one of the most attractive commercial vehicles currently on sale.

Standard-range models already offer impressive value, while spending around £2,500 more on the pre-registered market unlocks the Long Range version, increasing WLTP range to an impressive 258 miles. With a practical load area, comfortable cabin and Kia's seven-year warranty, the PV5 is a compelling alternative to more established electric vans. 


Mercedes-Benz eCitan
RRP From : £29,695 + VAT (£35,634 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £17,995 + VAT (£21,594 inc. VAT)

The Mercedes-Benz eCitan proves that a premium badge doesn't have to come with a premium price tag. Typical pre-registered examples undercut the equivalent ICE Citan by around £1,000, making the electric version the more compelling buy.

A WLTP range of up to 175 miles is more than sufficient for last-mile deliveries, urban businesses and local trades, while the refined driving experience and high-quality cabin help justify the Mercedes-Benz badge. For buyers seeking premium appeal without stretching their budget, the eCitan stands out as one of the most convincing small electric van bargains currently available.


Nissan Townstar
RRP From : £22,640 + VAT (£27,168 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £15,098 + VAT (£18,118 inc. VAT)


Peugeot E-Expert
RRP From : £28,905 + VAT (£34,686 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £16,994 + VAT (£20,393 inc. VAT)

The Peugeot E-Expert proves that switching to an electric medium van no longer requires a premium budget. Typical pre-registered examples undercut equivalent diesel models by around £1,000, giving buyers zero-emission driving for less money.

Offering medium van practicality at little more than the price of many compact vans, the E-Expert combines a modern, high-tech cabin with a sharper facelifted design that gives it a more desirable appearance. A WLTP range of up to 217 miles comfortably covers the needs of many businesses, making the E-Expert one of the strongest value electric van deals currently available.


Renault Trafic E-Tech
RRP From : £39,500 + VAT (£47,400 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £15,000 + VAT (£18,000 inc. VAT)

The Renault Trafic E-Tech is one of the best-value electric vans on the pre-registered market, with discounts of well over £20,000 from its original list price. It also undercuts the equivalent diesel model by almost £5,000, making the switch to electric an easy financial decision for many buyers.

A WLTP range of up to 183 miles comfortably covers typical business needs, while its load area remains one of the largest in the medium van sector, where many newer rivals have become slightly smaller.


Toyota Corolla Commercial
RRP From : £23,961.67 + VAT (£28,754 inc. VAT)
Pre-Reg Price : c. £17,992 + VAT (£21,590 inc. VAT)

The Corolla Commercial proves that a practical van doesn't need to sacrifice the comfort and efficiency of a passenger car. Based on the Corolla Touring Sports estate, which starts from £31,560, it combines Toyota's proven self-charging hybrid system with excellent fuel economy and a refined driving experience.

Toyota now offers an impressive six-model commercial vehicle range in the UK, while the Corolla Commercial provides a unique alternative to traditional small vans, blending car-like comfort with outstanding reliability and low running costs.


Vauxhall Combo
RRP From : £22,870 + VAT (£27,444 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £13,995 + VAT (£16,794 inc. VAT)


Volkswagen Caddy
RRP From : £25,825 + VAT (£30,990 inc. VAT)
Pre Reg From : c. £20,198 + VAT (£24,238 inc. VAT)

The Volkswagen Caddy combines car-like comfort, impressive refinement and a premium cabin with the practicality expected of a compact van. Closely related to the Ford Transit Connect, it is available with efficient diesel engines or a plug-in hybrid, with the latter typically costing around £2,500 more on the pre-registered market.

While not the cheapest option in its class, strong pre-registered discounts significantly reduce the premium often associated with German brands. For buyers seeking comfort, technology and a prestigious badge, the Caddy is now a far more convincing proposition.

Prices, specifications and availability were correct at the time of publication but may change without notice. Manufacturer recommended retail prices (RRPs) are shown excluding VAT, with VAT-inclusive prices provided for guidance at the current UK rate of 20%. Typical pre-registered prices are approximate UK market values and may vary depending on specification, location, seller and VAT status.
