Omoda Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Omoda is one of China's newest brands to enter the UK market, focusing on stylish SUVs packed with technology and generous equipment at highly competitive prices. Backed by Chery, one of China's largest automotive manufacturers, Omoda combines distinctive design with modern petrol, plug-in hybrid and electric powertrains.

Rather than competing purely on price, the brand aims to offer premium styling, strong value and impressive standard specification. With an expanding model range and competitive pricing, Omoda is rapidly establishing itself as a genuine alternative to both mainstream and premium manufacturers in the family SUV market.

Current Models

Omoda 5 (24-)

RRP From : £25,235
Pre Reg From : c. £19,500
Used Price From : c. £17,500

Powertrains : Petrol, EV
Dimensions : 4405 × 1830 × 1588 mm
Boot : 360L, 380L EV

Key rivals : Hyundai Kona, Kia XCeed, Mazda CX-30, MG ZS, Vauxhall Frontera

The Omoda 5 combines striking styling with generous equipment and competitive pricing, offering buyers the choice of petrol or fully electric power. A comfortable ride, modern technology and distinctive design help it stand out in the crowded family crossover market, while strong value makes it an increasingly attractive alternative to more established rivals.

Omoda 7 (25-)

RRP From : £35,235
Pre Reg From : c. £27,500
Used Price From : c. £26,000

Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4660 × 1875 × 1670 mm
Boot : 500L

Key rivals : Citroën C5 Aircross, Dacia Bigster, Geely Starray, Kia Sportage, MG HS

The Omoda 7 moves the brand into the heart of the family SUV market, combining an efficient plug-in hybrid powertrain with generous interior space, bold styling and an impressive level of standard equipment. Competitive pricing and a long electric driving range make it a compelling alternative to more established family SUVs.

Omoda 9 (25-)

RRP From : £44,990
Pre Reg From : c. £37,000
Used Price From : c. £32,000

Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4775 × 1920 × 1671 mm
Boot : 660L

Key rivals : BYD Seal U, Leapmotor C10 REEV, Mazda CX-60, Renault Rafale, Vauxhall Grandland

The Omoda 9 targets the upper end of the family SUV market with a spacious interior, high-quality cabin and a powerful plug-in hybrid drivetrain. Strong performance, generous equipment and a substantial boot help it offer exceptional value, while its premium design and refinement position it as a genuine alternative to far more expensive European rivals.
