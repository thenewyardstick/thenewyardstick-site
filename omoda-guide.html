Omoda Guide

Omoda is one of China's newest brands to enter the UK market, focusing on stylish SUVs packed with technology and generous equipment at highly competitive prices. Backed by Chery, one of China's largest automotive manufacturers, Omoda combines distinctive design with modern petrol, self-charging hybrid, plug-in hybrid and electric powertrains.

Rather than competing purely on price, the brand aims to offer premium styling, strong value and impressive standard specification. With an expanding model range and competitive pricing, Omoda is rapidly establishing itself as a genuine alternative to both mainstream and premium manufacturers in the family SUV market.

Current Models

Omoda 5 (24-)

RRP From : £24,040
Pre Reg From : c. £19,500
Used Price From : c. £15,500

Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid, EV
Dimensions : 4405 × 1830 × 1588 mm
Boot : 380 litres Petrol, 370 litres Hybrid / EV

Key rivals : Hyundai Kona, Kia XCeed, Mazda CX-30, MG ZS, Vauxhall Frontera

The Omoda 5 combines striking styling with generous equipment and competitive pricing, with petrol, self-charging hybrid and fully electric versions now available. The new hybrid broadens its appeal further, while the electric version provides a practical alternative for buyers wanting to move away from petrol. A comfortable ride, modern technology and distinctive design help it stand out in the crowded family crossover market.

Omoda 7 (26-)

RRP From : £29,965
Pre Reg From : c. £23,500
Used Price From : c. £23,500

Powertrains : Petrol, PHEV
Dimensions : 4660 × 1875 × 1670 mm
Boot : 639 litres Petrol, 590 litres PHEV

Key rivals : Citroën C5 Aircross, Dacia Bigster, Geely Starray, Kia Sportage, MG HS

The Omoda 7 moves the brand into the heart of the family SUV market, offering a choice of conventional petrol or plug-in hybrid power. Generous interior space, bold styling and an impressive level of standard equipment help it compete strongly with established family SUVs, while the plug-in hybrid combines useful electric driving capability with long-distance flexibility.

Omoda 9 (25-)

RRP From : £44,995
Pre Reg From : c. £35,000
Used Price From : c. £30,500

Powertrains : PHEV
Dimensions : 4775 × 1920 × 1671 mm
Boot : 660 litres

Key rivals : BYD Seal U, Leapmotor C10 REEV, Mazda CX-60, Renault Rafale, Vauxhall Grandland

The Omoda 9 targets the upper end of the family SUV market with a spacious interior, high-quality cabin and a powerful plug-in hybrid drivetrain. Strong performance, generous equipment and a substantial boot help it offer exceptional value, while its premium design and refinement position it as a genuine alternative to far more expensive European rivals.
