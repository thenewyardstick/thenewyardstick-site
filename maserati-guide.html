Maserati Guide

Maserati blends Italian style, handcrafted luxury and distinctive character in a way few manufacturers can match. Rather than chasing high sales volumes, the brand is increasingly focusing on lower-volume, higher-margin production, helping maintain exclusivity while supporting stronger long-term residual values.

The Grecale SUV, GranTurismo and MCPura showcase Maserati's broad appeal, while Folgore electric models demonstrate its commitment to electrification. With beautiful interiors, charismatic powertrains and a genuine sense of occasion, Maserati remains one of the automotive world's most distinctive luxury brands.

Current Models

Maserati Grecale (22-)

RRP From: £59,990
Pre Reg From: c. £50,000
Used Price From: c. £36,500

Powertrains: Petrol, MHEV, EV
Dimensions: 4846 × 1948 × 1670 mm
Boot: 535L

Key rivals: Alfa Romeo Stelvio, BMW X3, Land Rover Range Rover Velar, Porsche Cayenne, Volvo EX60

The Grecale replaces the larger Levante with a lighter, more agile approach, shedding over 300kg while remaining every bit as practical for most buyers. Elegant styling, a beautifully crafted interior and impressive refinement create a genuine sense of occasion, while six-cylinder Trofeo and electric Folgore models deliver astonishing performance. Competitive pricing and strong residual values suggest Maserati's lower-volume strategy is redefining the brand's reputation.

Maserati GranTurismo (23-)

RRP From: £127,920
Pre Reg From: c. £125,000
Used Price From: c. £90,000

Powertrains: Petrol, EV
Dimensions: 4959 × 1957 × 1353 mm
Boot: 310L, 270L (EV)

Key rivals: Aston Martin Vantage, Bentley Continental GT, Denza Z, Ferrari Amalfi

The GranTurismo remains one of the world's finest grand tourers, combining elegant Italian styling with genuine everyday usability and long-distance comfort. Available as both a coupé and GranCabrio convertible, petrol and Folgore electric versions offer buyers a choice of charismatic V6 power or cutting-edge electric performance. Few cars blend beauty, refinement and a sense of occasion quite as effortlessly.

Maserati MCPura (22-)

RRP From: £209,930
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: c. £160,000

Powertrains: Petrol
Dimensions: 4669 × 1965 × 1224 mm
Boot: 100L

Key rivals: Chevrolet Corvette ZR1, Ferrari 296 GTB, Lamborghini Temerario, McLaren Artura

The MCPura proves a 3.0-litre V6 can deliver genuine world-class supercar performance. Thanks to its carbon-fibre monocoque, lightweight design and innovative Nettuno engine, it rivals far more powerful machines while remaining engaging, beautifully balanced and unmistakably Italian. Few cars extract more performance from six cylinders alone.

Maserati GT2 Stradale (25-)

RRP From: £276,980
Pre Reg From: N/A
Used Price From: N/A

Powertrains: Petrol
Dimensions: 4669 × 1965 × 1222 mm
Boot: 100L

Key rivals: Ferrari 296 GTB, Lamborghini Temerario, McLaren 750S, Porsche 911 GT3 RS

The GT2 Stradale brings Maserati's modern racing programme directly to the road. Based on the same carbon-fibre architecture as the MCPura but developed with lessons from the GT2 race car, it combines a 640bhp Nettuno V6 with extensive aerodynamic upgrades, reduced weight and far more track-focused chassis tuning. With 0–62mph taking just 2.8 seconds and a 201mph top speed, it sits at the very top of Maserati's road-car range while remaining fully road legal.
