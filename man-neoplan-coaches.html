MAN / NEOPLAN Coaches



NEOPLAN Tourliner

The Tourliner is NEOPLAN's established British-market coach, combining distinctive Sharp Cut exterior styling with comfortable passenger accommodation and proven MAN mechanical components. Available in four body configurations, ranging from a relatively manageable 12.1-metre two-axle coach to a larger 13.9-metre three-axle model, it accommodates up to 63 passengers in manufacturer specifications. British operators commonly select 49- to 61-seat interiors, although more spacious executive layouts are available. Its 12.4-litre six-cylinder diesel engine delivers strong performance for motorway and touring work, while reclining seats, generous luggage storage and optional wheelchair access allow considerable versatility. Digital camera mirrors, modern safety systems and a redesigned cockpit further strengthen its appeal. New Tourliners continue to be delivered to British operators, including Maynes Coaches in 2026.

Important Information

Prices, specifications, dimensions, seating capacities, weights, power outputs and equipment are based primarily on information published by vehicle manufacturers, their official UK representatives and established suppliers. Specifications can vary by body length, chassis, drivetrain, configuration, model year and individual vehicle.

Prices shown are UK prices where available and are intended as a guide. Where an official UK list price is unavailable, pricing is shown as POA. Prices can vary substantially according to operator requirements, fleet contract size, optional equipment, accessibility provisions and specialist interiors.

Only models with evidence of current British-market availability are included. MAN and NEOPLAN manufacture additional buses and coaches for international markets, but their availability elsewhere does not confirm that they can currently be ordered new in the UK.

Passenger seating capacities vary according to body length, interior layout and accessibility equipment. Wheelchair positions, passenger toilets, additional luggage accommodation and executive seating may reduce the number of available passenger seats.

Licence categories shown are a general guide only. Individual driving entitlement can vary according to passenger-seat count, vehicle weight, vehicle length, licence issue date, grandfather rights, Section 19 operation and other exemptions. Passenger-seat counts used for licence purposes exclude the driver. Always check the vehicle's plated specification and your own driving-licence entitlement before driving.

Specifications are checked against the latest information reasonably available to The New Yardstick at the time of publication or update. Manufacturers and distributors may alter specifications, pricing or model availability without notice.
