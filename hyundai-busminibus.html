Hyundai Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

Hyundai's STARIA takes a distinctive approach to large passenger transport, combining sleek, futuristic styling with a spacious cabin and a noticeably more upmarket feel than many van-derived alternatives. High-quality materials, strong equipment levels and an airy interior give it the character of an oversized MPV rather than a conventional minibus.

Hyundai STARIA Electric

The STARIA Electric brings Hyundai's latest EV technology to its largest passenger vehicle, with an 84 kWh battery, rapid 800V charging and seating for up to nine people. Its flat electric floor, sliding side doors and flexible three-row cabin make it particularly well suited to larger families, airport transfers, hotels and private-hire work, while its car-like driving environment makes it considerably less intimidating than moving up to a conventional minibus.
