EVM / Cityliner Mid-Sized Buses & Minibuses

TNY 13 Sep 2026

EVM Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

EVM specialises in premium and accessible passenger vehicles, with a range spanning executive minibuses, low-floor city buses and larger midicoaches. The Grand Tourer turns the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter into a luxury minicoach for private hire, touring and corporate transport, while the Cityline uses the Sprinter as the basis for a low-floor bus designed around accessible urban and community services.


EVM Grand Tourer

The Grand Tourer is EVM's luxury Sprinter-based minicoach, designed primarily for longer-distance private hire, corporate travel, sightseeing and VIP transport. Coach-built bodywork creates more passenger and luggage space than a conventional Sprinter conversion, while panoramic double glazing, executive seating, passenger service units and a large rear luggage compartment give it much of the feel of a larger coach in a considerably smaller vehicle.

Price From: POA

Base Vehicle: Mercedes-Benz Sprinter

Powertrain: Diesel

Engine: Mercedes-Benz OM654 turbo diesel

Transmission: 9G-Tronic automatic

Seats: 11–19 passengers, plus driver

Typical Licence: D1 / D, depending on vehicle specification and entitlement

Luggage Capacity: Up to c.2.8 m³, specification dependent

Typical Use: Private hire, corporate transport, sightseeing, touring, airport transfers and VIP transport

EVM Cityline

The Cityline takes the Mercedes-Benz Sprinter in a very different direction, converting it into a compact low-floor bus for urban, community and accessible passenger transport. A wide passenger entrance and wheelchair ramp provide straightforward step-free boarding, while seating and standing layouts allow it to carry more passengers than a conventional Sprinter minibus. It is particularly suited to local bus routes, hospital and staff shuttles, community transport and other services where accessibility matters more than long-distance coach-style equipment.

Price From: POA

Base Vehicle: Mercedes-Benz Sprinter

Powertrain: Diesel

Engine: Mercedes-Benz OM654 turbo diesel

Transmission: 9G-Tronic automatic

Passenger Capacity: Up to 25 passengers

Typical Layouts: 18 seated + 4 standing / 16 seated + 9 standing

Typical Licence: D

Accessibility: Low-floor passenger area with wheelchair-accessible entrance and ramp

Wheelchair Capacity: Wheelchair space available, configuration dependent

Passenger Door: Wide powered sliding entrance door

Typical Use: Local bus routes, community transport, hospital and staff shuttles, accessible transport and demand-responsive services

EVM Electric Cityline

The Electric Cityline applies the same low-floor passenger concept to a fully electric Sprinter-based vehicle. Its accessible entrance, wheelchair provision and standing capacity make it a genuine small electric service bus rather than simply an electric passenger van. It is intended for predictable urban and shuttle routes where operators can make use of depot charging and zero-emission operation.
