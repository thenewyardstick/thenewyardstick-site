Kia Guide

Kia has transformed from a value-focused challenger into one of Europe's most complete car manufacturers, offering everything from compact city cars to large electric SUVs and commercial vehicles. Its range combines modern design, generous equipment and one of the industry's longest warranties with petrol, hybrid and electric powertrains to suit almost every buyer.

Strong value for money, impressive reliability and practical day-to-day usability have helped make Kia one of the UK's best-selling brands. Whether you're looking for a first car, spacious family SUV or capable electric vehicle, Kia now offers one of the broadest and most competitive ranges on sale today.
