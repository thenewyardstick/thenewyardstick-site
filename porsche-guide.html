Porsche Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Porsche today is a very different company from the one defined almost entirely by the 911. The sports car still anchors the brand, but most buyers now choose SUVs like the Macan and Cayenne, which have become everyday‑friendly, family‑usable staples. The shift to electrification is accelerating too: the Taycan has established Porsche as a serious EV player, while plug‑in hybrid versions of the Panamera and Cayenne are now core products rather than niche options. The modern Porsche range is broader, more practical and more technology‑heavy than ever, built around cars that are as capable on the school run as they are rewarding on a mountain road.

Current Models

Porsche Macan Electric (24-)

RRP From : £69,800
Pre Reg From : c. £69,800
Used Price From : c. £54,000

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4784 × 1938 × 1622 mm
Boot : 540L

Key rivals : BMW iX3, Genesis Electrified GV70, Maserati Grecale Folgore, Mercedes-Benz GLC EV, Volvo EX60

The Macan Electric brings Porsche's SUV formula into the EV era without dramatically changing what buyers already liked about the original. A spacious cabin, refined ride and impressive electric range are paired with sharp styling and everyday practicality, making it an appealing premium family SUV that still delivers the character buyers expect from a Porsche.

Porsche Cayenne (23-)

RRP From : £78,300
Pre Reg From : c. £75,000
Used Price From : c. £54,500

Powertrains : Petrol, PHEV, EV
Dimensions : 4930 × 1983 × 1674 mm
Boot : 648L, 627L (PHEV), 781L (EV)

Key rivals : Audi Q8, BMW X5, Land Rover Range Rover Sport, Mercedes-Benz GLE, Volvo XC90

The Cayenne remains Porsche's largest-volume family SUV, combining luxury, performance and genuine practicality in a way few rivals can match. Petrol, plug-in hybrid and future electric versions ensure it continues to evolve alongside changing buyer demands without abandoning the qualities that made it such a success.

 Porsche Taycan (20-)

RRP From : £88,465
Pre Reg From : c. £82,500
Used Price From : c. £29,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4963 × 1966 × 1381 mm
Boot : 407L

Key rivals : Audi e-tron GT, BMW i5, Lotus Emeya, MG IM5, Polestar 5

The Taycan is Porsche's first dedicated electric model - a four‑door that anchors the brand’s electric future. Its 800‑volt architecture delivers rapid charging and strong performance, but the Taycan’s story isn’t just about speed. It’s also one of the fastest‑depreciating EVs on the market, making it a compelling option to buy second‑hand: practical, usable, and now far more attainable than when new.

Porsche Panamera (24-)

RRP From : £91,100
Pre Reg From : c. £87,500
Used Price From : c. £66,000

Powertrains : Petrol, PHEV
Dimensions : 5052 × 1937 × 1423 mm
Boot : 494L, 430L PHEV

Key rivals : Audi A7, BMW 5-Series, Lexus ES, Mercedes E-Class, Polestar 5

The Porsche Panamera occupies a unique position, combining the comfort of a luxury saloon with the practicality of a hatchback. Long-range plug-in hybrid versions make it one of Porsche's most convincing electrified models, offering buyers the flexibility of petrol power alongside genuine electric-only capability.

Porsche 911 (19-)

RRP From : £105,200
Pre Reg From : c. £105,200
Used Price From : c. £62,500

Powertrains : Petrol, Hybrid
Dimensions : 4542 × 1852 × 1303 mm
Boot : 132L

Key rivals : Aston Martin Vantage, BMW M4, Chevrolet Corvette, Mercedes-AMG GT, MG Cyberster

The modern 911 offers one of the broadest sports car line-ups on sale, with everything from everyday Carrera models to extreme GT variants and new performance hybrids. While still rewarding to drive, it has become increasingly refined and practical over the years, offering occasional rear seats, enough luggage space for touring and the kind of comfort that makes daily driving feel entirely natural.
