MAXUS Minibuses & Passenger Vehicles

MAXUS has built a strong presence in the UK commercial-vehicle market, with the full-size DELIVER 9 providing a flexible base for specialist passenger conversions. Diesel and fully electric versions can be adapted for school, community, care, private-hire and shuttle work, while wheelchair-accessible layouts are also available through established UK converters.



MAXUS DELIVER 9 Minibus

The DELIVER 9 uses MAXUS's largest diesel van as the basis for a broad range of passenger conversions. Its boxy body provides plenty of usable cabin space, while specialist converters offer layouts for schools, charities, care organisations, community transport and private-hire work. Wheelchair-accessible versions can be specified with removable seating, passenger lifts and flexible interior layouts, giving operators considerable freedom to tailor the vehicle to different passenger-transport requirements.

MAXUS eDELIVER 9 Minibus

The eDELIVER 9 provides an electric alternative to the diesel DELIVER 9, retaining the large van body while replacing the combustion engine with a fully electric drivetrain. Specialist UK conversions are available for shuttle, community and wheelchair-accessible transport, making it particularly relevant for operators working in towns, cities and low-emission areas. A choice of battery sizes allows operators to balance range, payload and cost according to the intended route.
