Praga Guide

Founded in 1907, Praga is one of the Czech Republic's oldest vehicle manufacturers, with a history spanning road cars, racing machinery, trucks, motorcycles and even aircraft. Although the company spent decades away from mainstream passenger-car production, it continued developing its engineering expertise through motorsport, particularly with lightweight racing cars and competition karts.

The Bohema marks Praga's return to road-going cars, combining a carbon-fibre structure, extreme aerodynamic performance and a Nissan-derived V6 engine developed with British specialists Litchfield.

Rather than pursuing outright horsepower figures, Praga concentrates on keeping weight exceptionally low while delivering genuine racing-car characteristics. British customers can order through official dealer Premier GT, with right-hand drive available.

The Bohema is a road-legal, track-focused hypercar that combines a 700bhp 3.8-litre twin-turbocharged V6 with a six-speed sequential gearbox and rear-wheel drive. Derived from the Nissan GT-R's engine and extensively developed by Litchfield, the powertrain sits within a lightweight carbon-fibre structure weighing less than 1,000kg. Despite its extreme appearance, the Bohema seats two adults and provides 100 litres of luggage storage across compartments behind the doors.
