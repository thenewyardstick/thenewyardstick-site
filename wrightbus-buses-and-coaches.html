Wrightbus StreetDeck Electroliner

The StreetDeck Electroliner is Wrightbus's fully electric double-decker, designed for demanding urban and regional bus services. The second generation, introduced in 2025, replaces earlier battery technology with a more efficient 442kWh lithium iron phosphate system, providing a claimed range of up to 275 miles. Faster charging and improved battery durability make the bus easier to integrate into intensive daily schedules, while seating for up to 73 passengers ensures it retains the practicality expected of a conventional double-decker. The Electroliner shares much of its technology with the single-deck GB Kite, simplifying maintenance for operators running both types. An electric open-top StreetDeck is also offered for sightseeing services, although its published battery specifications differ from the latest enclosed model.

Wrightbus StreetDeck Hydroliner

The StreetDeck Hydroliner is Wrightbus's hydrogen fuel-cell double-decker, offering zero-tailpipe-emission operation without relying on large traction batteries or lengthy depot charging. Its latest specification combines a 70kW Ballard fuel cell with a 111kWh battery, using hydrogen to generate electricity for the drivetrain. Wrightbus quotes a range of more than 300 miles and refuelling in approximately eight minutes under suitable conditions. The updated model also benefits from improved drivetrain efficiency and component commonality with the single-deck GB Kite Hydroliner. With capacity for up to 90 passengers, including standing accommodation, it provides an alternative to battery-electric buses where hydrogen infrastructure is available and intensive daily operation makes rapid refuelling attractive.

Wrightbus StreetDeck Ultroliner

The StreetDeck Ultroliner remains Wrightbus's conventional diesel double-decker, providing a practical option for operators unable to move immediately to electric or hydrogen buses. The latest generation introduces a Cummins 6.7-litre six-cylinder diesel engine with up to 300hp, paired with a seven-speed Voith automatic gearbox. A Mercedes-Benz four-cylinder diesel remains another published option. Three body lengths offer seating for up to 83 passengers, with a maximum total capacity of 100 in selected layouts. Modern passenger equipment and accessibility features make the Ultroliner suitable for busy public transport routes, while Wrightbus's NewPower programme provides a possible future route to battery-electric conversion. New examples entered service in Liverpool during September 2026.

Wrightbus GB Kite Electroliner & Hydroliner

The GB Kite is Wrightbus's modern single-deck bus family, available with either battery-electric or hydrogen fuel-cell propulsion. The second-generation Electroliner introduces CATL lithium iron phosphate batteries with capacities of 442kWh or 528kWh, with Wrightbus claiming up to approximately 365 miles from the larger battery. The Hydroliner instead generates electricity using a hydrogen fuel cell, with several hydrogen storage configurations and a claimed maximum range of up to 640 miles under favourable conditions. Both versions feature low-floor passenger access, flexible seating and standing areas, and modern passenger equipment. They share many components with Wrightbus's StreetDeck double-deckers, helping operators simplify parts inventories and maintenance across mixed fleets.

Wright Contour

The Contour marks Wrightbus's return to the touring-coach market after more than three decades, reviving a name originally used during the 1980s. Introduced in March 2025, the new model offers up to 55 reclining passenger seats within a 12.2-metre body, with USB charging, modern safety equipment and substantial luggage accommodation. A 400hp Cummins diesel engine and ZF automatic transmission provide the power, while operators can choose PSVAR-compliant, prepared or conventional interiors. The Contour is marketed in Britain and Europe, with an initial order for 39 coaches from Ireland's Bus Éireann scheduled for delivery during 2026. Wrightbus also has a hydrogen touring coach under development, although a confirmed current production model has not yet been established.

Important Information

Prices, specifications, dimensions, seating capacities, weights, power outputs and equipment are based primarily on information published by vehicle manufacturers and their official UK representatives. Specifications can vary by body length, drivetrain, interior layout, model year and individual operator requirements.

Prices shown are UK prices where available and are intended as a guide. Where an official UK list price is unavailable, pricing is shown as POA. Prices can vary according to contract size, optional equipment, accessibility requirements and operator-specific configurations.

Passenger seating capacity must not be confused with total passenger capacity. Urban buses may accommodate additional standing passengers, while wheelchair spaces, door arrangements and other interior equipment can affect the number of available seats.

Electric vehicle battery capacities, driving ranges and charging times are manufacturer claims based on stated conditions. Real-world results depend on passenger load, operating speed, weather, heating, route characteristics and battery condition. Maximum charging power may require optional equipment and suitable infrastructure.

Hydrogen fuel-cell vehicle ranges and refuelling times depend on storage configuration, hydrogen pressure, station capability and operating conditions. Fuel-cell buses produce no tailpipe carbon dioxide emissions during normal hydrogen operation, but the overall environmental impact depends on how the hydrogen is produced and supplied.

Licence categories shown are a general guide only. Individual driving entitlement can vary according to passenger-seat count, vehicle weight, vehicle length, licence issue date, grandfather rights, Section 19 operation and other exemptions. Passenger-seat counts used for licence purposes exclude the driver. Always check the vehicle's plated specification and your own driving-licence entitlement before driving.

Specifications are checked against the latest information reasonably available to The New Yardstick at the time of publication or update. Manufacturers may alter specifications, prices, equipment or availability without notice. Wrightbus NewPower conversions are separate from factory-new vehicle sales.
