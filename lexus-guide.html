Lexus Guide

TNY 17 Jul 2026

Lexus has built its reputation on exceptional refinement, reliability and comfort, offering a distinctive alternative to Europe's established premium brands. Rather than focusing on outright performance, its range prioritises smooth, quiet driving, meticulous build quality and low running costs, with hybrid technology forming the backbone of the lineup for more than two decades.

Today, that expertise is being carried into a growing range of fully electric models, while flagship vehicles such as the RX, ES and LM continue to demonstrate the brand's commitment to effortless long-distance comfort. Whether you're looking for a compact crossover or luxury chauffeur vehicle, Lexus remains one of the automotive industry's safest premium choices

Current Models

Lexus LBX (25-)

RRP From : £29,995
Pre Reg From : c. £29,500
Used Price From : c. £23,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4190 × 1825 × 1560 mm
Boot : 402L

Key rivals : Alfa Romeo Junior, Audi Q2, Ford Puma, MINI Aceman, Toyota Yaris Cross

The LBX brings Lexus quality and refinement to the compact crossover class. Its efficient hybrid powertrain, premium interior and comfortable ride make it feel more expensive than its size suggests, while low running costs and excellent reliability should appeal to buyers looking for an upmarket small SUV.

Lexus UX (19-)

RRP From : £38,095
Pre Reg From : £38,095
Used Price From : c. £12,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 4495 × 1840 × 1540 mm
Boot : 320L

Key rivals : Audi Q3, BMW X1, Honda ZR-V, Peugeot 3008, Renault Austral, Volvo XC40

The UX is beginning to show its age, with the newer and smaller LBX surprisingly offering significantly more boot space. However, it remains a refined, reliable and efficient premium crossover, combining excellent comfort, low running costs and an easy-going driving experience.

Lexus NX (21-)

RRP From : £46,695
Pre Reg From : £46,695
Used Price From : c. £22,000

Powertrains : Hybrid, PHEV
Dimensions : 4660 × 1865 × 1670 mm
Boot : 545L, 520L PHEV

Key rivals : BMW X3, DS No7, Range Rover Velar, Renault Rafale, Volvo XC60

The NX is one of the strongest all-round premium family SUVs, blending comfort, quality and efficiency. The plug-in hybrid offers an excellent electric range, while both versions provide a beautifully finished interior and the refinement Lexus has become known for.

Lexus RZ (23-)

RRP From : £48,365
Pre Reg From : c. £36,500
Used Price From : c. £34,500

Powertrains : EV
Dimensions : 4805 × 1895 × 1635 mm
Boot : 522L

Key rivals : Audi Q6 e-tron, DS No8, Ford Mustang Mach-E, MG IM6, Polestar 4

Positioned as the fully electric alternative to the RX, the RZ combines Lexus refinement with battery-electric power. Quiet, comfortable and exceptionally well built, it favours effortless cruising over outright performance. Competitive pricing and excellent pre-registered value make it one of the more attainable premium electric SUVs, while Lexus' strong reputation for reliability adds further long-term appeal.

Lexus RX (23-)

RRP From : £63,445
Pre Reg From : c. £60,500
Used Price From : c. £34,500

Powertrains : Hybrid, PHEV
Dimensions : 4890 × 1920 × 1695 mm
Boot : 612L, 461L PHEV

Key rivals : Audi Q8, Maserati Grecale, Mercedes-Benz GLE, Range Rover Sport

Now in its fifth generation, the RX remains a cornerstone of the brand, helping establish its reputation for refined SUVs while defining the premium hybrid SUV long before many rivals entered the segment. Spacious, beautifully built and prioritising comfort over outright handling, it's available with efficient hybrid powertrains, making it an outstanding luxury family SUV.

Lexus ES (26-)

RRP From : £59,345
Pre Reg From : £59,345
Used Price From : £59,345

Powertrains : Hybrid, EV
Dimensions : 5140 × 1920 × 1555 mm
Boot : 377L

Key rivals : BMW i5, Denza Z9 GT, Mercedes-Benz EQS, Polestar 5, Volvo ES90

The latest ES moves Lexus firmly into the larger end of the executive saloon market, combining hybrid and fully electric powertrains with the comfort and refinement that have defined previous generations. A spacious cabin, elegant styling and smooth driving experience make it an appealing choice for buyers wanting executive luxury with modern powertrains.

Lexus LM (24-)

RRP From : £97,645
Pre Reg From : c. £80,000
Used Price From : c. £73,000

Powertrains : Hybrid
Dimensions : 5130 × 1890 × 1945 mm
Boot : 752L (4-seat)

Key rivals : Mercedes-Benz V-Class, Toyota Proace Verso, Volkswagen Multivan

The LM is less a people carrier and more a luxury lounge on wheels. Designed primarily for chauffeur use, it offers exceptional rear-seat comfort, limousine-like refinement and one of the most luxurious cabins available at any price. It occupies a unique niche in the UK market and has few direct rivals.
